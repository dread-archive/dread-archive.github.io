DA-0102 Book · 1987

Tomie

Junji Ito · Japan

Dread 4/5

A beautiful girl who is murdered again and again. Every piece of her grows back into another Tomie.

Tomie Kawakami is beautiful, cruel and irresistible. The men around her fall in love with her and, sooner or later, kill her. It never lasts. She returns, and any piece of her left behind can grow into a new Tomie, each of whom hates the others.

Junji Ito’s first published series, which began in 1987, grew into a long run of connected stories. It was later the basis for a series of films.

It is a horror story about obsession that turns the usual victim into the thing that cannot die. Ending her is exactly what keeps her going.
