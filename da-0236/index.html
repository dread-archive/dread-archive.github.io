DA-0236 Game · 2010

Amnesia: The Dark Descent

Frictional Games · Sweden

Dread 5/5

No weapons, no fighting back, and a protagonist who erased his own memory for a reason.

A man named Daniel wakes up in the dark halls of Brennenburg Castle in 1839, remembering only his name. He finds a note in his own handwriting. It says he chose to forget, and that he must go down into the castle and kill the baron who lives there.

Frictional Games’ horror game gives the player no means of defence. Daniel can only hide, run, and avoid looking at what hunts him, because the dark and the monsters wear down his sanity.

The most disturbing thing in the game is not in the dark. It is the letter. Daniel’s past self knew exactly what he had done, and decided the present one should not have to carry it. The game asks whether that was mercy.
