DA-0293 Game · 2019

Blair Witch

Bloober Team · Poland

Dread 4/5

A former policeman joins the search for a missing boy in the Black Hills Forest, with only his dog for company.

It is 1996. Ellis, a former police officer haunted by his past, drives to the woods near Burkittsville, Maryland, to help look for a missing child. He goes in alone with his dog, Bullet. Soon the radio fails, the paths stop leading where they should, and he finds a camcorder with tapes that seem to change the forest around him.

The Polish studio Bloober Team, known for Layers of Fear, made it as a licensed game set in the world of the 1999 film The Blair Witch Project.

The dog is its best idea. Bullet can find trails, and he reacts to things Ellis cannot see. Watching him grow afraid is more frightening than any monster. Under the forest is a story about guilt and trauma that the woods seem to know about.
