DA-0237 Game · 2017

Hellblade: Senua's Sacrifice

Ninja Theory · United Kingdom

Dread 4/5

A warrior travels to the land of the dead to save the soul of the man she loved, and the voices come with her.

Senua, a Pictish warrior, journeys into a Norse underworld carrying the head of her dead lover, hoping to bargain for his soul. She experiences psychosis. Throughout the game, voices whisper around her, sometimes helping, sometimes mocking, sometimes warning her of danger. She sees patterns in the world that may not be there.

Ninja Theory worked with neuroscientists and people with lived experience of psychosis, and recorded the voices binaurally, so that on headphones they seem to come from all around the player’s own head.

It is a combat game on the surface, but its real subject is perception. The player learns, as Senua does, to live with voices that will not stop.
