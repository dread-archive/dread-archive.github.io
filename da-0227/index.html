DA-0227 Game · 2007

BioShock

Irrational Games (2K Boston / 2K Australia) · United States / Australia

Dread 3/5

In 1960, a plane crashes in the Atlantic. The only survivor swims to a lighthouse, and goes down to the city under the sea.

Jack survives a plane crash in mid-ocean and finds a lighthouse with a bathysphere inside. It takes him to Rapture, a city built on the seabed by the industrialist Andrew Ryan for people who refused all limits. The city has collapsed into civil war. Its citizens, deformed by genetic modification, roam its ballrooms and shopping arcades, and armoured giants guard small girls who harvest the dead.

Ken Levine’s team made it as a spiritual successor to System Shock 2. The art deco city, with its advertising and slogans, was its main achievement.

Its horror is the scale of a ruined idea. Every room records a utopia’s last days, in tape recordings left by people who believed in it.
