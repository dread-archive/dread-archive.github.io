DA-0310 Broadcast · 1977

Children of the Stones

HTV (Jeremy Burnham and Trevor Ray) · United Kingdom

Dread 4/5

A children's serial about a village inside a stone circle, where everyone is very, very happy.

An astrophysicist and his teenage son move to the village of Milbury, which sits inside a prehistoric stone circle, to study it. The villagers are friendly, polite and unnervingly content, and they greet each other with the same phrase. The few who are not happy do not stay unhappy for long.

Made for British children’s television and shot in the real stone circle at Avebury, the serial is remembered by many who saw it as the most frightening thing they watched as children. Its score, a choir of dissonant voices, is as unsettling as the story.

It is a founding work of folk horror on television, and its idea, that a place might slowly absorb the people who live in it, has been borrowed many times since.
