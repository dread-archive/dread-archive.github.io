DA-0136 Film · 2008

The Strangers

Bryan Bertino · United States

Dread 4/5

A couple's night at a holiday house is interrupted by a knock at four in the morning. Someone is asking for Tamara.

After a friend’s wedding, Kristen and James return to his family’s summer house. The evening has gone badly. At four in the morning a young woman knocks and asks whether Tamara is home. Then three masked figures begin, patiently, to play with them.

Bryan Bertino’s debut is almost a two-hander, and it gets much of its fear from waiting: a masked figure standing in the background of a shot, unnoticed, for a very long time.

Its most quoted line is the answer to the question “why are you doing this?” It is the most frightening possible answer, because it is not really an answer.
