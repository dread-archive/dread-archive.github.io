DA-0210 Game · 1999

Resident Evil 3: Nemesis

Capcom · Japan

Dread 4/5

A former police officer tries to escape a city overrun by the dead. Something large is following her by name.

Jill Valentine, a survivor of the mansion incident in the first Resident Evil, is still in Raccoon City as it collapses. The streets are full of the infected and the police are gone. Umbrella has also sent something after the remaining members of her old unit: a huge, scarred creature in a trench coat, which can open doors, fire a rocket launcher, and keeps saying a single word.

Capcom released it for the PlayStation, with fixed camera angles and pre-rendered backgrounds like its predecessors. It added a dodge move and occasional choices under time pressure.

Nemesis changed the series. He is not a boss to be fought once but a presence that might come through any door, and that is a far more lasting kind of fear.
