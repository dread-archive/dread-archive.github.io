DA-0300 Game · 2017

Faith

Airdorf Games · United States

Dread 4/5

A young priest returns at night to the house where an exorcism failed a year ago, armed with a crucifix and very few pixels.

John Ward, a young Catholic priest, comes back to the remote Martin house, where a year earlier he took part in an exorcism on a girl named Amy that went badly wrong. He walks the grounds and the woods with only a crucifix, which he holds up against whatever he meets. Notes and strange figures suggest that more is happening here than one possession.

Airdorf Games released the first chapter in 2017. It is drawn to look like a game from the Atari 2600 era, with blocky sprites and a tiny palette, and voices in harsh synthesised speech.

The primitive style is what makes it work. Its brief, jerky animated scenes, far more detailed than the rest of the game, arrive without warning. The player’s imagination fills in the darkness between the pixels, and does not stop there.
