DA-0392 Film · 2023

Evil Dead Rise

Lee Cronin · United States / New Zealand

Dread 4/5

An earthquake opens a vault under a Los Angeles apartment building, and a mother becomes something else.

Beth, a guitar technician, visits her older sister Ellie, a single mother of three living in a run-down apartment block that is about to be demolished. An earthquake cracks open an old bank vault in the basement. Ellie’s son finds a strange book and some old records, and plays them.

Lee Cronin’s entry in the Evil Dead series moves the story from a cabin in the woods to a tower block, and keeps its mix of extreme violence and dark comedy. Its most frightening idea is simple: the person who is supposed to protect the children is now the thing they need protecting from.

It is loud, gory and relentless, with inventive uses of a cheese grater and a peephole.
