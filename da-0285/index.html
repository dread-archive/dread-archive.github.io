DA-0285 Game · 2015

The Park

Funcom · Norway

Dread 3/5

A mother runs back into an amusement park at closing time to find her son, who has gone after his lost teddy bear.

Lorraine and her young son Callum are leaving the Atlantic Island Park at the end of the day when he runs back through the gates after his teddy bear. She follows him in. The park is closing, the lights are coming on, and she can hear him calling but cannot reach him. She rides the attractions to look for him, narrating her thoughts as she goes.

Funcom made it as a short first-person game set in the world of its online role-playing game The Secret World. It takes about an hour and a half.

There is little danger in the usual sense. The horror is Lorraine herself: her exhaustion, resentment and guilt as a single mother, which come out more and more as the park darkens. It is a game about the thoughts a parent is ashamed of having.
