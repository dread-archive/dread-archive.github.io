DA-0315 Broadcast · 1987

The Max Headroom broadcast signal intrusion

Unknown · United States

Dread 3/5

Ninety seconds of someone else's signal on two Chicago stations, and the people behind it were never found.

On the evening of 22 November 1987, the sports segment of a Chicago news broadcast was interrupted by a figure in a Max Headroom mask, rocking in front of a moving corrugated background, with no usable sound. Engineers regained control within seconds.

About two hours later, the same figure appeared on another Chicago station during an episode of Doctor Who, this time with distorted audio, for about a minute and a half. It ended with a bizarre and unsettling scene that has been described, and speculated about, ever since.

The perpetrators were never officially identified. Nothing about the intrusion is supernatural. It belongs here because of the feeling it leaves behind: a channel you thought was one-way, and someone else on it.
