DA-0386 Film · 2022

Nope

Jordan Peele · United States

Dread 3/5

Siblings on a horse ranch try to film the thing in the sky. The hardest thing is not to look at it.

After their father is killed by debris falling from the sky, OJ and Emerald Haywood struggle to keep their family’s business going, training horses for film and television in a California valley. Something is hiding in a cloud that never moves. They decide to get it on camera and sell the footage.

Jordan Peele’s third film is a big, strange spectacle about spectacle itself: about the need to capture the unbelievable on camera, and what that desire costs. A disturbing subplot about a children’s television show, and a chimpanzee, sits at its heart.

Its creature is one of the most original in recent horror, and its rules are simple. Do not look up.
