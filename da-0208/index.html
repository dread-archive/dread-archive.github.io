DA-0208 Game · 1998

Resident Evil 2

Capcom · Japan

Dread 4/5

A rookie cop and a college student arrive in a city that has already fallen. The sequel that went bigger and kept its nerve.

On his first day on the job, police officer Leon Kennedy drives into Raccoon City and finds it overrun. He meets Claire Redfield, who is looking for her brother. They are separated, and each must find a way through the police station, the sewers and the laboratory underneath, while something much larger follows them.

Capcom expanded everything from the first Resident Evil (DA-0207) while keeping its tension. Each character has a separate campaign, and choices in one affect the other.

A remake in 2019 rebuilt the game with a close camera and a relentless pursuer who follows the player through the station, and it is often called one of the best remakes ever made.
