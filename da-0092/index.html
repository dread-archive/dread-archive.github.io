DA-0092 Film · 2003

A Tale of Two Sisters

Kim Jee-woon · South Korea

Dread 4/5

Two sisters return home to their father and a cold stepmother. Something else in the house remembers.

Two sisters, Su-mi and Su-yeon, come home from a stay in a psychiatric hospital to a beautiful, isolated country house. Their father is distant. Their new stepmother is brittle and hostile. Strange things happen at night, and the house seems to hold a secret about the girls’ mother.

Kim Jee-woon based the film on an old Korean folk tale and shot it with lush colour and patterned wallpaper, like an unsettling fairy tale. It was a major international success and helped build the reputation of Korean horror abroad.

Its structure is a puzzle, and the answer is in the grief of the characters, not the ghosts. It rewards a second viewing, when you know which memories to trust.
