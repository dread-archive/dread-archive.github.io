DA-0187 Book · 1987

Beloved

Toni Morrison · United States

Dread 3/5

A house in Ohio is haunted by a baby. Then a young woman walks out of the water and comes to stay.

It is 1873. Sethe, who escaped from slavery in Kentucky, lives with her daughter Denver at 124 Bluestone Road outside Cincinnati, a house shaken by an angry ghost. When Paul D, a man she knew on the Sweet Home plantation, arrives and drives the spirit out, a strange young woman appears soon after, soaked, calling herself Beloved.

Toni Morrison drew on the case of Margaret Garner, a woman who escaped slavery in 1856. The novel won the Pulitzer Prize in 1988.

It uses the shape of a ghost story to carry a history that ordinary realism could not hold. Beloved’s hunger for Sethe’s attention grows without limit, and what she represents cannot be argued with or forgotten.
