DA-0148 Film · 2015

The Invitation

Karyn Kusama · United States

Dread 4/5

A man goes to a dinner party at the house where he used to live, hosted by his ex-wife and her new friends.

Will and his girlfriend drive into the Hollywood Hills for a dinner with old friends. It is hosted by Eden, Will’s former wife, in their old house. Two years ago their son died there. Eden and her new partner seem calm, happy, and eager to show everyone a video about the group that helped them.

Karyn Kusama keeps the tension at the level of social discomfort for most of the film. The doors are locked “because of the neighbourhood”. Nobody wants to be the rude guest.

It is a film about how far good manners will carry us into danger, and about grief that has been offered an easy answer.
