DA-0036 Book · 1872

Carmilla

Sheridan Le Fanu · Ireland

Dread 3/5

The vampire story that came a quarter-century before Dracula, and is in many ways stranger.

Laura lives with her father in a remote castle in Styria. After a carriage accident on the road outside, a young woman named Carmilla is left in their care. The two girls become close. Carmilla is lovely, languid and oddly secretive. At the same time, the young women of the surrounding villages begin to sicken and die.

Le Fanu’s novella was first serialised in 1871–72 and collected in In a Glass Darkly. It predates Stoker’s Dracula by twenty-five years, and Stoker drew on it.

One of its best details is how the visitor hides in plain sight. The same woman appears across generations under different names, Mircalla, Millarca and Carmilla, each built from the same letters. Anyone who reads the names side by side can see who she is. For most of the book, nobody does.
