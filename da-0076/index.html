DA-0076 Film · 1984

A Nightmare on Elm Street

Wes Craven · United States

Dread 4/5

A killer who attacks teenagers in their dreams. You cannot stay awake forever.

In a quiet American suburb, teenagers begin having the same dream: a burned man in a striped sweater and a battered hat, with blades on the fingers of one glove. Then they start dying in their sleep. Nancy realises that the only way to survive is to stop sleeping, and then to find out what her parents did years ago.

Wes Craven built the film around a simple, cruel idea: the one place nobody can protect you is your own sleep. Its early scenes, in which dreams and waking blur without warning, are its most frightening.

Freddy Krueger went on to become a wisecracking franchise star. In the first film he rarely jokes. He waits, because sooner or later everyone has to close their eyes.
