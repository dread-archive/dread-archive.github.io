DA-0007 Book · 1959

The Haunting of Hill House

Shirley Jackson · United States

Dread 5/5

The best haunted-house novel ever written, because the house is only half of it.

Four people come to stay in a house that does not want them. One of them, Eleanor, begins to feel that it wants her very much.

Dr. Montague has invited them to study the house. Theodora is bright and teasing. Luke will inherit the place one day. Eleanor has spent most of her adult life caring for her mother, and has never had anywhere to belong. Hill House offers her somewhere. That is the trap.

Jackson almost never shows you anything. Doors close. Something knocks along a corridor. Writing appears on a wall. The fear comes from Eleanor’s voice, which grows warmer toward the house as the reader grows colder. By the end it is impossible to say where the haunting stops and she begins, and the book does not intend you to be able to.
