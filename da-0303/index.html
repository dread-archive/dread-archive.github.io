DA-0303 Broadcast · 1959

The Twilight Zone

Rod Serling · United States

Dread 3/5

Half-hour moral fables with a twist, introduced by their creator. The template for televised strangeness.

Each episode of Rod Serling’s anthology series tells a self-contained story in which an ordinary person finds that the rules of the world have changed: a town where everyone suspects each other, a man who finally has time to read, a passenger who sees something on the wing of a plane that no one else can. Serling introduced each story himself, standing calmly at the edge of the frame.

The series used science fiction and fantasy to talk about paranoia, conformity, war and prejudice at a time when television rarely did.

Many of its twist endings are now so familiar they have become jokes. The episodes still work because Serling cared more about the people than the twist, and because he made the strange feel like it was happening next door.
