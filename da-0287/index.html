DA-0287 Game · 2016

Dead by Daylight

Behaviour Interactive · Canada

Dread 3/5

Four survivors try to repair generators and escape while a fifth player, the killer, hunts them through the fog.

Each match places four survivors in a foggy arena such as a farm, a hospital or a junkyard. They must repair enough generators to power the exit gates and get out. A fifth player controls the killer, who hunts them in first person, carries the wounded to meat hooks, and offers them to a dark power known as the Entity.

The Canadian studio Behaviour Interactive released it in 2016. It has since added many characters, including licensed killers and survivors from well-known horror films.

It turns the slasher film into a game of nerves between real people. Survivors hear a heartbeat when the killer is near, and must decide whether to hide, run, or risk themselves to rescue someone else. The fear comes from knowing the hunter is thinking too.
