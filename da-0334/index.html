DA-0334 Broadcast · 2015

Limetown

Zack Akers and Skip Bronkie (Two-Up Productions) · United States

Dread 3/5

A radio reporter investigates a research town in Tennessee where more than three hundred people disappeared overnight.

Ten years ago, every resident of Limetown, a small community in Tennessee built around a neuroscience research facility, vanished. The emergency services were blocked from entering. Lia Haddock, a reporter for American Public Radio, has a personal connection to the town, and begins a podcast investigation.

Zack Akers and Skip Bronkie created it, modelled on the format of true-crime podcasts, and released the first season in 2015. It later became a novel and a television series.

It sounds like a documentary, with interviews, archive recordings and narration, and this gives it its first chill. Its second comes from the subject: a scientific experiment in connecting human minds, and what such an experiment would do to the people inside it.
