DA-0154 Film · 2007

Paranormal Activity

Oren Peli · United States

Dread 3/5

A couple sets up a camera in their bedroom at night, and a timestamp becomes the most frightening thing on screen.

Katie believes she has been followed by a presence since childhood. Her boyfriend, Micah, buys a video camera to record what happens in their suburban house at night. He is enthusiastic and sceptical. The camera sits on a tripod at the foot of the bed.

Oren Peli made the film on a tiny budget in his own house. After festival screenings and a word-of-mouth campaign it was released widely in 2009 and became one of the most profitable films ever made.

Almost nothing happens, and that is the technique. The audience learns to scan the dark bedroom while the timestamp in the corner runs through the small hours. A door moves an inch. The audience holds its breath. The camera keeps recording whatever comes.
