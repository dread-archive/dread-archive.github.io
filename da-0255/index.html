DA-0255 Game · 2015

Until Dawn

Supermassive Games · United Kingdom

Dread 3/5

An interactive slasher film in which any of the eight friends can die, depending on you.

A year after two sisters vanished on Blackwood Mountain, eight friends return to the remote lodge where it happened. They intend to spend a night remembering them. Something else is on the mountain.

Supermassive Games built the game like an interactive horror film, with a large cast and a branching story driven by the player’s choices. Any character can survive or die. The game tracks its “butterfly effect” choices and shows the player how small decisions ripple outward.

It knowingly plays with slasher-film clichés, then twists them. Between chapters, an unsettling psychiatrist questions the player directly about their fears. It is a reminder that the game is taking notes.
