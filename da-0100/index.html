DA-0100 Film · 2003

Ju-On: The Grudge

Takashi Shimizu · Japan

Dread 5/5

A curse is born in a house where someone died in rage. Everyone who enters takes it with them.

A care worker visits a house to look after an elderly woman and finds a small boy taped inside a cupboard. A cat meows from somewhere it cannot be. The film follows her, and everyone else who has been inside the house, in short chapters told out of order.

Takashi Shimizu had already made two low-budget video versions of the story before this theatrical film. The pale mother and son at its centre, and the croaking sound that announces them, became some of the most copied images in horror.

There is no rule and no way to break the curse. The house simply remembers, and what it remembers spreads.
