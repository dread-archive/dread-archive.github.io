DA-0320 Broadcast · 2014

Unedited Footage of a Bear

Alan Resnick and Ben O'Brien (Adult Swim) · United States

Dread 4/5

It begins as nature footage and then a medication advertisement. Then it becomes something much worse.

The video starts with shaky footage of a bear in the woods. It cuts to what looks like an advertisement for an allergy medication, in which a woman takes a pill and goes about her day. Then the advertisement will not end. She realises something is wrong with her house, and with the other woman who seems to be living in it.

Made for Adult Swim’s late-night slot, and broadcast without explanation, the film was created by Alan Resnick and Ben O’Brien, part of a group that made several similar pieces.

It uses the language of ordinary late-night television so accurately that viewers who stumbled on it could not tell, at first, what they were watching. By the time they could, it was too late to look away.
