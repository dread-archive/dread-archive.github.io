DA-0375 Game · 2021

Resident Evil Village

Capcom · Japan

Dread 3/5

A father searches a snowy village for his kidnapped daughter, and meets its four lords.

Ethan Winters, the everyman hero of Resident Evil 7 (DA-0262), has rebuilt his life with his wife and baby daughter. Then his daughter is taken, and he wakes in a remote village in the mountains of Europe, ruled by a mysterious figure called Mother Miranda and her four lords, each with a territory: a castle, a doll maker’s house, a reservoir and a factory.

The game borrows from gothic horror, werewolf films and folk tales, and each area feels like a different kind of horror. Its castle and its towering, elegant vampire lady became instant favourites.

Its best section is the doll maker’s house, which removes all weapons and becomes a pure, quiet haunted-house experience.
