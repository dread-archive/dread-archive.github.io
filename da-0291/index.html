DA-0291 Game · 2004

Michigan: Report from Hell

Grasshopper Manufacture (Goichi Suda) · Japan

Dread 3/5

A television crew goes out into a city covered by a strange mist. The player is the cameraman, and decides what to film.

A thick mist has covered Chicago, and a news crew from the station ZaKa TV goes out to report on it. The player never appears on screen. Everything is seen through the cameraman’s lens, and the crew’s reporter and sound engineer react to what he points it at.

It was made by Grasshopper Manufacture under Goichi Suda, better known as Suda51, and released in Japan for the PlayStation 2 in 2004 and in Europe the following year. It was never released in North America.

It is a rough, uneven game, but its idea is uncomfortable. The game rates the footage, and some of what it rewards is the footage a decent person would not take. The horror is partly the creatures in the mist, and partly the job of recording them.
