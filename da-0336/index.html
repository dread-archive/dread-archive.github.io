DA-0336 Broadcast · 2017

Petscop

Anonymous (YouTube) · United States (internet)

Dread 4/5

A YouTube series about playing an unreleased video game that keeps revealing things it should not contain.

In 2017, a YouTube channel began posting videos of someone named Paul playing Petscop, an apparently unreleased PlayStation game he says he found. It looks like a cheerful children’s puzzle game about catching pets. Paul quickly finds hidden areas that do not look cheerful at all.

Two dozen videos were posted between 2017 and 2019. The hidden areas contain fragments of a story about a real-seeming family, a missing child, and a set of rules and rituals. Viewers built enormous theories to decode it.

Petscop is a landmark of internet horror, and a model for mysteries that reward close watching. Its lasting unease comes from the idea that a piece of software could contain someone’s private history, and that the person playing it seems to know more than he says.
