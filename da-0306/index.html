DA-0306 Broadcast · 2016

Channel Zero

Nick Antosca · United States

Dread 4/5

A television anthology that turned internet horror stories into slow, strange seasons of TV.

Each season of Nick Antosca’s series adapts a different creepypasta, the short horror stories passed around online, and expands it into a full, very loose story. The first season, based on Candle Cove (DA-0350), follows a child psychologist returning to his hometown, where a strange puppet show on television decades ago coincided with a series of child murders.

Later seasons adapted other internet stories, about an endless house, a staircase in the woods, and a door that should not be opened.

The series was praised for its patience and its disturbing designs, and for trusting its audience to live with ambiguity. Its version of Candle Cove’s puppets is not easily forgotten.
