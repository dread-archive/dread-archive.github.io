DA-0219 Game · 2001

Fatal Frame

Tecmo · Japan

Dread 4/5

The only weapon is a camera, so the only defence is to keep looking.

Miku Hinasaki goes into the abandoned Himuro Mansion to find her brother, who went there looking for someone else. She finds an antique camera, the Camera Obscura, which can capture and harm the spirits that live there.

Released in Europe as Project Zero, the game turns a basic horror instinct inside out. You cannot look away. To fight a ghost you must raise the viewfinder and wait for it to come closer, because the closer it is, the more damage the photograph does.

The mansion’s history unfolds through diaries and rituals, and the atmosphere is quiet and oppressive. It is a game about bearing witness, and about how much a single frame can hold.
