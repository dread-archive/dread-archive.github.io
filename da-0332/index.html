DA-0332 Folklore · 2006

The Rake

Anonymous (online) · Internet

Dread 3/5

A pale, naked, crouching figure that appears at the end of people's beds, and a collection of accounts from those who saw it.

The Rake is described as a hairless, emaciated, humanoid thing that moves on all fours and has long claws. In the accounts, it appears in bedrooms at night, sitting at the foot of the bed and staring at the sleeper. Sometimes it leaves. Sometimes it does not.

It began on internet forums in the middle of the 2000s as a deliberate attempt to create a new monster, and spread through a written collection of supposed eyewitness reports, diary entries and a suicide note, all claiming to describe it across several centuries.

It owes its power to the plain, documentary tone of its sources. Its fear is very simple, close to sleep paralysis: waking to find someone already in the room, who has been watching for some time.
