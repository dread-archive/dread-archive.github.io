DA-0345 Folklore · 1735

The Jersey Devil

Traditional (New Jersey Pine Barrens) · United States

Dread 2/5

The legend of the thirteenth child of the Pine Barrens, and a creature that became a regional mascot.

According to the legend, in 1735 a woman known as Mother Leeds, pregnant with her thirteenth child, cursed it in exasperation. The child was born and then transformed: hooves, a forked tail, the wings of a bat. It flew up the chimney and into the Pine Barrens of southern New Jersey, where it has lived ever since.

Stories of the creature have circulated for centuries, but the most famous wave of sightings came in January 1909, when newspapers reported strange tracks and encounters across the region.

The legend is mostly affectionate now. The state’s professional ice hockey team is named after it. But the Pine Barrens are still vast, dark and nearly empty, and the story fits them perfectly.
