DA-0365 Broadcast · 2016

Archive 81

Daniel Powell and Marc Sollinger · United States

Dread 3/5

An archivist is hired to digitise old tapes in an isolated facility. The tapes are about an apartment building, and about him.

Dan Powell takes a job restoring a collection of audio tapes at a remote facility. The tapes were made in the 1990s by a woman called Melody, who was interviewing the residents of the Visser, an apartment building in New York. As he listens, Dan becomes fixed on her, and on what happened in that building.

Daniel Powell and Marc Sollinger created the podcast, presented as recordings Dan made and sent to a friend. The first season appeared in 2016, and it later inspired a television series.

It builds slowly, with tenants, a cult-like community and the sound of rooms that should not exist. Its central fear is about listening: that by paying attention to a recording, one may be allowing something on it to pay attention back.
