DA-0406 Film · 2025

Sinners

Ryan Coogler · United States

Dread 3/5

Twin brothers open a juke joint in 1930s Mississippi. The music draws in something that wants to join.

In 1932, twin brothers return to their hometown in the Mississippi Delta with money from Chicago and buy an old mill to open a juke joint for one night. Their young cousin is a gifted blues guitarist whose playing, the film suggests, is powerful enough to call across time. As the night goes on, strangers arrive at the door, asking to be let in.

Ryan Coogler’s film is a period drama, a musical and a vampire film at once, with Michael B. Jordan playing both brothers. Its central musical sequence, in which music from many eras seems to fill the room, is extraordinary.

It is a vampire story about community and exploitation, and about outsiders who want to take something that is not theirs, very politely.
