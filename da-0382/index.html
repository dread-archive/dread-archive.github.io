DA-0382 Film · 2021

The Medium

Banjong Pisanthanakun (director), Na Hong-jin (producer) · Thailand / South Korea

Dread 5/5

A documentary crew follows a village shaman in north-eastern Thailand as her niece begins to change.

A film crew is making a documentary about Nim, a shaman in rural north-eastern Thailand who is the vessel of a local goddess. The role passes down the women of her family. When her niece, Mink, begins behaving strangely, the family wonders if the goddess is choosing her. The crew keeps filming as it becomes clear that something else is.

Made as a mockumentary, the film uses night-vision cameras, interviews and handheld footage, and it becomes increasingly relentless as the crew refuses to stop recording.

It is rooted in the religious practices of the region, and in the idea that a family’s past sins do not stay in the past. Its final act is among the most intense in recent horror.
