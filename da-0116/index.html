DA-0116 Film · 2004

Marebito

Takashi Shimizu · Japan

Dread 4/5

A freelance cameraman obsessed with fear follows it underground, and brings something back with him.

Masuoka is a freelance cameraman in Tokyo who films everything. After recording a stranger’s terrified face in the subway, he becomes fixated on what that man saw, and follows the trail into tunnels beneath the city, where he finds a vast underground world and a silent young woman in chains. He takes her home.

Takashi Shimizu shot it quickly on video between Ju-On films. The texture is cheap and cold, and much of it is seen through Masuoka’s own camera and the monitors in his flat.

It is a portrait of someone trying to learn fear by recording it, and never quite noticing what that is doing to him.
