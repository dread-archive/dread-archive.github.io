DA-0371 Game · 2021

Inscryption

Daniel Mullins Games · United States

Dread 3/5

A card game in a cabin that turns out to be something much stranger, and very aware of you.

You sit across a table from a figure in the dark who wants to play a card game. The cards are animals. Some of them talk. If you lose, the figure has something unpleasant in mind for you. You try to find a way out of the cabin between games.

To say much more would spoil it. The game has more layers than it first admits: the cabin, a story about a found piece of software, and a real person who went looking for something hidden in it.

It is a horror game about games, and about the files underneath them. It rewards the kind of player who opens folders they were not meant to open.
