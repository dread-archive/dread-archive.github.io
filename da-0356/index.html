DA-0356 Folklore · 1972

The Loveland Frog

Reported sightings · United States

Dread 2/5

Police officers in an Ohio town reported a large frog-like creature standing by the road at night.

In March 1972, a police officer driving near the Little Miami River in Loveland, Ohio, reported seeing a creature about four feet tall with a frog-like face cross the road in his headlights and climb over a guardrail. A second officer reported a similar sighting weeks later. Earlier local stories described several such creatures by a bridge.

The second officer later said he had seen a large lizard and that the story had been exaggerated. The legend remained anyway, and the town now celebrates it.

It is a gentle piece of folklore, more strange than frightening. But the image of something standing upright where no animal should, briefly caught in the headlights, is the root of many ghost stories.
