DA-0241 Game · 2014

P.T.

Kojima Productions (as 7780s Studio) · Japan

Dread 5/5

An hour-long teaser for a game that was never made, withdrawn and still whispered about.

You wake up on a concrete floor and walk into an ordinary suburban hallway: a clock, a radio, a door to a bathroom. At the end of the hallway is a door that leads back to the start of the same hallway. Each time around, something has changed.

P.T. was released as a free “playable teaser” on the PlayStation Store. Players who finished it learned that it announced a new Silent Hill game. That game was cancelled, and the teaser was removed from the store. It survives only on the consoles of people who downloaded it.

It is one hallway, and it is one of the most frightening places in games, because it teaches you every detail and then changes one. The ghost of the house, Lisa, is rarely seen clearly. You learn to listen for her.
