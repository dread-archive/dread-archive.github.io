DA-0119 Film · 2005

Noroi: The Curse

Kōji Shiraishi · Japan

Dread 5/5

A found-footage film assembled from a missing researcher's last documentary, and one of the most frightening of its kind.

The film presents itself as the final documentary by Masafumi Kobayashi, a paranormal researcher who investigated strange events: a neighbour complaining of babies crying, a young actress with psychic abilities, a man who covers his house in tin foil. At first the cases seem unrelated. Slowly, they connect.

Kōji Shiraishi built the film from talk-show clips, interviews and handheld footage, and the patchwork looks convincingly real. Its mystery unfolds like a puzzle the viewer assembles alongside the researcher.

The dread builds from small details: a name that keeps recurring, a ritual in an old village, a shape at the edge of the frame. By the time the pieces come together, the viewer understands why the documentary was never finished.
