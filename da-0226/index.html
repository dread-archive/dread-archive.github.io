DA-0226 Game · 2005

Pathologic

Ice-Pick Lodge · Russia

Dread 4/5

Three healers arrive in a town on the steppe as a plague begins. They have twelve days.

A small town on the Russian steppe, built around a slaughterhouse and a strange tower, is struck by the Sand Plague. The player chooses one of three healers: a metropolitan doctor, the son of a local surgeon trained in the steppe’s folk medicine, or a strange girl who claims to heal by touch. Over twelve days, they must try to save the people they can.

Ice-Pick Lodge, a Moscow studio, released it as Mor. Utopia in Russian. It is famously punishing: food is scarce, prices rise, and time runs out on most of the town’s people regardless.

The game refuses heroism. The plague moves from district to district, children play strange games in the streets, and the real choice is always who to let die.
