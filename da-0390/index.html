DA-0390 Film · 2018

Possum

Matthew Holness · United Kingdom

Dread 4/5

A disgraced children's puppeteer returns to his childhood home in Norfolk, carrying a bag he cannot get rid of.

Philip, a children’s entertainer, returns to the decaying house where he grew up, now occupied by his unsettling stepfather, Maurice. He carries a leather bag holding a puppet: a spider with a pale human head. He tries again and again to dispose of it in the fields and rivers around the house. It keeps finding its way back.

Matthew Holness wrote and directed it, his first feature, developed from his own short story. Sean Harris plays Philip and Alun Armstrong plays Maurice. Its music is by the Radiophonic Workshop.

It has very little dialogue and almost no explanation. The Norfolk landscape is grey and empty. Its horror is in a memory that cannot be thrown away, and a figure from childhood that is still waiting in the house.
