DA-0163 Film · 2016

Ouija: Origin of Evil

Mike Flanagan · United States

Dread 3/5

In 1967 a widow who runs a fake séance business adds a Ouija board to the act. Her younger daughter turns out to be good at it.

Alice Zander, a widow in Los Angeles, makes a living holding séances with the help of her two daughters, hidden wires and a lot of theatre. It comforts her clients, she tells herself. When she buys a Ouija board, her youngest, Doris, starts answering questions nobody should know the answers to.

Mike Flanagan’s prequel is far better than the film it follows, with careful period detail, down to the cigarette burns in the corner of the frame.

It is a story about a harmless con that becomes something real, and a family that only notices when it is too late.
