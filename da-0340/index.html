DA-0340 Folklore · 1981

Polybius

Urban legend · United States

Dread 3/5

An arcade cabinet that probably never existed, with a legend that has outlived every real game of its year.

According to the legend, in 1981 a new arcade game called Polybius appeared in a few arcades in the suburbs of Portland, Oregon. Players became obsessed. Some had nightmares, memory loss and seizures. Men in black suits were seen visiting the machines, not to collect coins but to collect data. Then the cabinets disappeared.

No cabinet, photograph, or record of a manufacturer has ever been found. The story appears to have first been written up online around 2000, and has been retold, expanded, and parodied ever since.

It survives because it fits a fear that has only grown: that the machine you are playing with is also playing with you, and that someone is quietly writing down the results.
