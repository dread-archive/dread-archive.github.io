DA-0376 Broadcast · 2021

Midnight Mass

Mike Flanagan (Netflix) · United States

Dread 4/5

A charismatic young priest arrives on a dying island, and the miracles begin.

A disgraced young man returns home to a small fishing island with a shrinking population. At the same time, a young priest arrives to replace the island’s elderly monsignor, who has not returned from a trip. Soon there are miracles: a girl who could not walk stands up, the old grow younger. The island’s faith grows stronger, and the priest has a secret.

Mike Flanagan’s miniseries is long, patient and full of monologues about faith, death and addiction. Its horror arrives slowly and then very fast.

It is a story about how a community can talk itself into anything when it is desperate, and how a promise of eternal life can be the most dangerous gift of all.
