DA-0258 Game · 1992

Night Trap

Digital Pictures; created by Rob Fulop and James Riley · United States

Dread 1/5

A player watches hidden cameras in a lakeside house and springs traps to protect a group of teenage girls from intruders.

A group of young women arrive for a sleepover at the Martin family’s lakeside house. The player is an operator for a special police team, watching through cameras hidden around the house, and triggering traps to catch the black-clad creatures, called Augers, who are trying to drain the guests’ blood.

The video was filmed in 1987 for a never-released Hasbro console and finally appeared in 1992 on the Sega CD. Its stars included Dana Plato. It became infamous when it was raised during the 1993 United States Senate hearings on violence in video games.

It is mostly camp, and rarely frightening. Its lasting horror is structural: the player is always looking at the wrong room, and hears what happened somewhere else only afterwards.
