DA-0190 Game · 1992

Alone in the Dark

Infogrames (Frédérick Raynal) · France

Dread 3/5

A detective explores a haunted Louisiana mansion, and the survival horror game is born.

After the owner of Derceto, a mansion in Louisiana, hangs himself, a private investigator or the owner’s niece goes to the house to investigate. The attic is already occupied. The house is full of creatures from Lovecraft’s fiction, and full of books that explain them, some of which are dangerous to read.

Frédérick Raynal’s game placed three-dimensional polygon characters over pre-drawn backgrounds and shifted between fixed, cinematic camera angles. It was the first game to combine exploration, puzzles, limited resources and horror in the way the genre later called survival horror.

Its polygons look crude now. The design does not. Many rooms are safe, and the game makes you unsure which ones.
