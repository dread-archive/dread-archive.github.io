DA-0246 Game · 2015

SOMA

Frictional Games · Sweden

Dread 5/5

The most unsettling game ever made about minds, memory and copies.

Simon Jarrett goes in for a brain scan in 2015. He wakes up in 2104 in PATHOS-II, an underwater research facility at the bottom of the Atlantic. The surface is gone. The machines around him seem to believe they are people.

Frictional Games, the studio behind Amnesia, made a horror game that is mostly a philosophical argument, told through corridors, radio messages and difficult choices. The monsters are frightening. The questions are worse.

The game’s central idea is simple and hard to shake. When a mind is copied, both copies wake up believing they are the original, and only one of them is right. SOMA never lets the player look away from what that means.
