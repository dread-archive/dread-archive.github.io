DA-0205 Game · 1995

D

Kenji Eno (WARP) · Japan

Dread 3/5

A young woman arrives at a hospital where her father has killed many people, and walks into a castle that should not exist.

Laura Harris receives news that her father, a doctor at a Los Angeles hospital, has killed patients and staff and barricaded himself inside. She goes in and finds herself in a vast medieval castle, where her father is waiting. She must explore it and find out why.

Kenji Eno and his small studio WARP made the game first for the 3DO console. It used pre-rendered full-motion video, and it had no pause and no save: the whole game must be finished within two hours of real time, or it ends.

The time limit gives a cinematic game the pressure of a nightmare. The castle is lavish and strange, but the real dread is the story Laura is slowly piecing together about her family.
