DA-0274 Game · 2009

Silent Hill: Shattered Memories

Climax Studios · United Kingdom

Dread 3/5

A father searches a snowbound town for his daughter after a car crash, while a psychologist asks the player questions.

Harry Mason wakes beside his crashed car in Silent Hill, in heavy snow, and his daughter Cheryl is gone. He searches the town with a phone that receives strange calls and messages. Between chapters, the player sits with a psychologist, Dr. Kaufmann, answering questions and filling in tests.

Climax Studios, a British developer, reimagined the first Silent Hill for the Wii. It removed combat entirely: in the nightmare sequences the town freezes over and the player can only run and hide from the creatures.

The game watches what the player does and adapts characters, clothes and locations to what it learns. Its horror is the experience of being observed, and then of seeing the game’s portrait of you take shape.
