DA-0301 Broadcast · 1938

The War of the Worlds (radio broadcast)

Orson Welles / The Mercury Theatre on the Air (CBS) · United States

Dread 2/5

The broadcast that taught everyone how easily a story wearing the clothes of the news can travel.

On the night before Halloween in 1938, CBS broadcast a radio adaptation of H. G. Wells’s novel. Written by Howard Koch and directed by Orson Welles, it was performed as a series of news bulletins interrupting a music programme, reporting a landing at Grover’s Mill, New Jersey.

The programme said it was a drama at the start and at other points during the hour. Some listeners who tuned in partway through were frightened anyway. How widespread the panic really was has been debated ever since. Newspapers of the time almost certainly exaggerated it.

It belongs in this archive because of what it proved: format is persuasive. A voice that sounds like the news is believed a little before it is heard.
