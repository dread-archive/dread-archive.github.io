DA-0339 Folklore · 1970

Annabelle

Anonymous (popularised by Ed and Lorraine Warren) · United States

Dread 3/5

A Raggedy Ann doll, given as a present in 1970, is said to have moved by itself, and now sits in a glass case with a warning.

According to the story, a nursing student was given a large Raggedy Ann doll as a gift around 1970. She and her roommate began to find it in different positions and different rooms, and to discover notes they had not written. A medium told them the doll was inhabited by the spirit of a dead girl called Annabelle.

The case was publicised by the paranormal investigators Ed and Lorraine Warren, who took the doll away and kept it in a glass case in their occult museum in Connecticut, with a sign telling visitors not to open it. A much more frightening-looking version later appeared in the Conjuring films.

There is no evidence for any of it. But the legend’s appeal is old and simple: a child’s toy, smiling and harmless, that is never quite where it was put.
