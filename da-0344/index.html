DA-0344 Folklore · 1950

The Hook

Urban legend · United States

Dread 2/5

The classic lovers' lane legend, and the template for thousands of stories told at sleepovers.

A young couple is parked on a dark road. The radio announces that a killer has escaped from a nearby asylum, identifiable by the hook he has in place of one hand. The girl wants to leave. The boy is annoyed but drives her home. When he gets out to open her door, something is hanging from the handle.

Folklorists have recorded versions of the story across the United States since at least the 1950s. The details change and the ending never does.

It is a perfect small machine: a warning, a near miss, and an object left behind as proof. Almost everyone who tells it heard it first as something that happened to a friend of a friend.
