DA-0319 Broadcast · 2019

Gemini Home Entertainment

Remy Abode · United States

Dread 4/5

A series of old VHS tapes from a home video company, warning about things in the woods and in the sky.

Each video presents itself as a recovered VHS tape from a company called Gemini Home Entertainment: a guide to identifying wildlife, a lesson on the planets, a safety video. The narration is calm and helpful. The advice is increasingly strange, and the creatures described in it do not exist.

Remy Abode’s series was one of the key works of the analog horror wave on YouTube, alongside Local 58 (DA-0322) and The Mandela Catalogue (DA-0366). It uses the look of degraded tape and the tone of educational video to create a creeping sense of a world ending quietly, off camera.

Its strength is its voice: the soothing presenter who never raises his tone, even when explaining what to do if the stars go out.
