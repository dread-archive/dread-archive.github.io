DA-0363 Folklore · 1998

The Black-Eyed Children

Internet (first account by Brian Bethel) · United States (internet)

Dread 3/5

A modern legend about quiet children with entirely black eyes who ask to come inside.

In the late 1990s, a journalist named Brian Bethel described an encounter in Abilene, Texas. Two boys knocked on his car window asking for a ride, and he felt an overwhelming dread before noticing that their eyes were completely black. He wrote about it online, and the story spread.

Similar accounts followed. They follow a pattern: the children are polite and insistent, they need to be let in, into a car or a house, and whoever meets them feels an irrational terror before understanding why.

The legend draws on old traditions about beings who cannot enter without an invitation. Its power is in how ordinary it is. It’s just a knock at night and a reasonable request, and everything depends on whether you say yes.
