DA-0380 Folklore · 1979

Kuchisake-onna

Traditional (Japan) · Japan

Dread 3/5

The slit-mouthed woman of Japanese legend, who asks a question that cannot be answered safely.

The legend describes a woman wearing a surgical mask who approaches people, often children walking alone, and asks, “Am I pretty?” If they say yes, she removes the mask to reveal a mouth slit from ear to ear and asks again. Most versions agree that there is no simple right answer.

The figure has roots in older tales, but the modern legend spread across Japan in 1979, when rumours of sightings reached such a pitch in some areas that schools reportedly had children walk home in groups.

It is a legend built entirely on a conversation. The monster does not chase you at first. She asks you something, and waits for the answer.
