DA-0058 Film · 1968

Rosemary's Baby

Roman Polanski · United States

Dread 4/5

Paranoia filmed in sunlight: a woman surrounded by helpful people, none of whom she can trust.

Rosemary and Guy Woodhouse move into a grand old New York apartment building. Their elderly neighbours are eccentric and very friendly. When Rosemary becomes pregnant, everyone takes a close interest in her health.

Polanski adapted Ira Levin’s 1967 novel almost scene for scene. Mia Farrow plays Rosemary as bright and increasingly isolated, as the people around her explain away every symptom, every sound through the wall, every doubt.

The film’s horror is social rather than supernatural. It is about being handled and managed and told what is good for you, and about how difficult it is to be believed when the people who could believe you are the ones you are afraid of.
