DA-0194 Game · 1993

The 7th Guest

Trilobyte (Rob Landeros and Graeme Devine) · United States

Dread 2/5

A toymaker invites six guests to his mansion for a night of puzzles. None of them leave.

The player wakes inside the mansion of Henry Stauf, a drifter turned toymaker whose toys were followed by the deaths of the children who owned them. Moving through the house room by room, the player solves logic puzzles, a cake, a chessboard, a microscope, and watches ghostly video fragments of the six guests who came here one night and competed for Stauf’s favour.

Trilobyte built it for the new CD-ROM drive, using pre-rendered 3D rooms and live actors filmed against blue screen. Published by Virgin Games, it was one of the products that sold CD-ROM drives to households.

It is mostly camp, with a sneering host. But the empty rooms, the moving camera and the sense of one thing everyone in the house is trying not to say give it a lingering chill.
