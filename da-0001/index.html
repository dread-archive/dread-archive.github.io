DA-0001 Film · 1922

Nosferatu

F. W. Murnau · Germany

Dread 3/5

A century old, and its shadow still climbs the stairs faster than the man who casts it.

An estate agent travels to the Carpathian mountains to sell a house to Count Orlok. The Count buys it. Then he comes to live in it, and he brings the plague with him.

Murnau made an unauthorised version of Dracula, and Bram Stoker’s widow sued. The court ordered every print destroyed. Some copies survived anyway, which is the most frightening thing about the film: it was sentenced to disappear, and it did not.

Max Schreck’s Orlok does not seduce anyone. He is thin and patient and wrong. The famous image is not the Count himself but his shadow on a staircase wall, arriving ahead of him. The film understands something later vampire stories forgot. The monster did not break in. He was sold the house.
