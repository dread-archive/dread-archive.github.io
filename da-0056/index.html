DA-0056 Film · 1968

Witchfinder General

Michael Reeves · United Kingdom

Dread 4/5

A witch-hunter tours a lawless English countryside, and the horror is entirely human.

During the English Civil War, with order collapsed, Matthew Hopkins travels through the villages of East Anglia with his assistant, finding witches wherever he is paid to. He was a real historical figure. In the film, played by Vincent Price with rare restraint, he is a self-appointed official who uses the law as cover for cruelty. A young soldier whose fiancée falls into Hopkins’s hands sets out for revenge.

Michael Reeves was twenty-four when he made the film, and died the following year. It was attacked for its violence on release and is now seen as a founding work of British folk horror.

There is nothing supernatural in it. That is its point. The monsters carry papers, collect fees, and are welcomed into town.
