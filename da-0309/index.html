DA-0309 Broadcast · 1941

The Hitch-Hiker

Lucille Fletcher · United States

Dread 4/5

A man driving across America keeps seeing the same hitch-hiker by the road, and it is not possible.

Ronald Adams sets out to drive from New York to California. Near the Brooklyn Bridge, he notices a shabby man trying to hitch a ride. Later that day, he sees the same man by the road again, hundreds of miles on. And again the next day, further still, always ahead of him, always waiting.

Lucille Fletcher wrote the radio play for Orson Welles, who performed it several times. It was later adapted for The Twilight Zone (DA-0303).

It is a simple, perfectly shaped story, told almost entirely as one man’s increasingly frightened monologue. Its final phone call is one of the most famous moments in radio drama.
