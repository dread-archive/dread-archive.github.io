DA-0366 Broadcast · 2021

The Mandela Catalogue

Alex Kister · United States

Dread 5/5

Analog horror at its most disturbing: public safety broadcasts about beings that imitate the people you love.

Set in Mandela County, Wisconsin, the series is presented as old broadcasts, home recordings and emergency messages. They warn residents about “alternates,” beings that look exactly like people and try to get into their homes. The advice is simple and awful: do not let them in, even if they look like your family.

Alex Kister’s web series became one of the most influential works of analog horror, known for its distorted faces, calm institutional voices and heavy religious imagery.

At its core is the oldest fear there is, sharpened by technology: that the person at the door is not the person they appear to be, and that you might not be able to tell.
