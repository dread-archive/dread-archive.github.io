DA-0368 Film · 2019

The Lighthouse

Robert Eggers · United States / Canada

Dread 4/5

Two lighthouse keepers on a rock in a storm, and the slow erosion of one man's mind.

In the 1890s, two men arrive to tend a lighthouse on a remote island off New England for four weeks. The older keeper guards the lamp at the top of the tower and will not let his young assistant near it. The younger man does the hard, filthy work and has strange visions. When a storm prevents their relief from arriving, the days stop being countable.

Robert Eggers shot the film in black and white in a nearly square frame, with dialogue built from period dialects and sea stories. Willem Dafoe and Robert Pattinson give ferocious performances.

It is funny, grotesque and full of myth. Its central image, a light at the top of the stairs that one man hoards and the other must see, is the whole film in one shape.
