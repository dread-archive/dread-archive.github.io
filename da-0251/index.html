DA-0251 Game · 2012

SCP – Containment Breach

Joonas Rikkonen · Finland

Dread 4/5

A test subject is led into a chamber with a concrete statue. It can only move when nobody is looking at it.

The player is D-9341, a test subject at a secret facility run by the SCP Foundation. Escorted into a containment chamber with other prisoners, they watch a statue known as SCP-173. The lights fail. When the subject escapes, the whole facility is in breach, and its other anomalies are loose.

Joonas Rikkonen built it for free, based on the collaborative SCP Foundation wiki, which began in 2008 and catalogues fictional anomalous objects in a dry clinical style. Its levels are partly generated at random.

Its most famous mechanic is a blink meter. The player must periodically blink, and SCP-173 moves only in the dark between. Something as ordinary as closing one’s eyes becomes the most dangerous thing a person can do.
