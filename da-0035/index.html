DA-0035 Book · 1954

I Am Legend

Richard Matheson · United States

Dread 4/5

The last man alive in a city of vampires, and one of the most influential horror novels ever written.

After a pandemic, Robert Neville appears to be the last uninfected man in Los Angeles. Everyone else has become a vampire. By day he repairs his barricaded house and hunts them in their sleep. By night they gather outside and call his name. He drinks, remembers his wife and daughter, and slowly begins studying the disease in a library, looking for a scientific cause.

Richard Matheson’s short novel mixes horror with grief and loneliness, and its ending turns the whole book around. The title means something very specific by the final page.

It has been filmed at least three times, and its image of a lone survivor besieged by the transformed dead shaped the zombie film as we know it. None of the adaptations has kept the original ending intact.
