DA-0222 Game · 2003

Manhunt

Rockstar North · United Kingdom

Dread 4/5

A man is executed, wakes up alive, and is told by a voice in his earpiece that his life now depends on how well he kills for the camera.

James Earl Cash is executed by lethal injection in Carcer City, and wakes up alive. A man who calls himself the Director speaks to him through an earpiece. Cash must move through the city’s rotting districts while gangs hunt him, and kill them quietly and brutally on camera, for a film the Director is making.

Rockstar North, best known for Grand Theft Auto, built it as a stealth game. The Director was voiced by Brian Cox. It was banned in several countries.

Its horror is less in the killings than in the frame around them. The Director grows excited when they are more cruel, and the player is the one watching every scene the camera records.
