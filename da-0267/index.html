DA-0267 Game · 2004

Kuon

FromSoftware · Japan

Dread 3/5

In Heian-era Kyoto, young sorcerers enter a nobleman's mansion where a ritual to cure his sickness has gone wrong.

In ancient Kyoto, the Fujiwara family’s mansion has fallen silent. Two young female onmyōji, Utsuki and Sakuya, enter it separately and find servants transformed, insects everywhere, and the traces of a ceremony intended to keep someone alive. A third chapter follows the famous sorcerer Abe no Seimei.

FromSoftware released it on the PlayStation 2 a decade before its Souls games became famous. The characters fight with spells drawn from Japanese folk magic, using paper charms and summoned beings, in slow, dim interiors.

Its pacing is stiff, but its setting is unusual in games: rice-paper screens, oil lamps and long verandas. Its horror is quiet, old and specifically Japanese, rooted in the idea that a curse spreads through a household like an illness.
