DA-0149 Film · 2014

Goodnight Mommy

Veronika Franz and Severin Fiala · Austria

Dread 4/5

Twin boys wait at a lakeside house for their mother. She comes home after surgery, bandaged, and different.

Elias and Lukas spend a hot summer at their modern house in the Austrian countryside. When their mother returns from cosmetic surgery, her face wrapped in bandages, she is cold and strict, and she will not acknowledge one of them. The twins begin to suspect she is not their mother at all.

The film, originally Ich seh, Ich seh, is clean, bright and very quiet. It is directed by Veronika Franz and Severin Fiala.

Its fear is an old one: the familiar person who comes back slightly wrong. And its turn makes the viewer ask whose perception they have been trusting.
