DA-0393 Film · 2023

Talk to Me

Danny Philippou and Michael Philippou · Australia

Dread 4/5

Teenagers pass around an embalmed hand at parties to let spirits in for ninety seconds. Someone holds on too long.

Mia, a teenager still grieving her mother’s death two years earlier, goes to a party where friends are playing a game with a ceramic-covered embalmed hand. You light a candle, hold the hand and say the words, and a spirit appears to you. Say the next words, and it enters you. Everyone films it for social media. The rule is to let go within ninety seconds.

The Philippou brothers, known for their YouTube channel, made their feature debut with a film that treats possession like a party drug: exciting, social and addictive.

Its horror comes from how appealing the game is. The spirits are frightening, but the teenagers keep coming back, because for ninety seconds they feel something.
