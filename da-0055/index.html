DA-0055 Film · 1968

Night of the Living Dead

George A. Romero · United States

Dread 4/5

The modern zombie begins here, and so does the modern ending.

A group of strangers barricade themselves in a Pennsylvania farmhouse while the dead walk outside. They board up the windows and argue about the cellar. They never find out why.

Romero made the film independently near Pittsburgh on a very small budget, and invented most of the rules the genre still uses. Duane Jones plays Ben, the calm centre of the house, and the film never explains or apologises for casting him.

The real threat is not the crowd at the windows. It is the people inside, who cannot agree on anything. The final minutes are among the bleakest in American horror, and they are not about the dead at all.
