DA-0196 Game · 2003

Siren

Sony Computer Entertainment Japan (Keiichiro Toyama) · Japan

Dread 5/5

A village swallowed by a red sea, and a game that lets you see through the eyes of the things hunting you.

An earthquake strikes a remote mountain village during a secret ritual, and the village is cut off by a sea of red water. Its people become the shibito, the risen dead, who continue their daily routines while hunting the living. The story is told out of order, through the overlapping days of ten survivors.

Directed by Keiichiro Toyama, who had worked on the first Silent Hill (DA-0209), the game’s central mechanic is sightjacking: the player can tune into the vision of nearby creatures, and see what they see, including themselves.

It is famously difficult and deliberately confusing. Its structure rewards patience, and the view of your own hiding place through the eyes of something searching for you is not easily forgotten.
