DA-0275 Game · 2012

Ib

kouri · Japan

Dread 3/5

A young girl visiting an art gallery with her parents finds the building empty and the paintings awake.

Ib, a nine-year-old girl, visits an exhibition by the painter Weiss Guertena with her parents. While she looks at a large painting, the lights flicker and everyone disappears. She steps into the painting’s world, where her life is tied to a rose: each injury costs a petal.

The game was made by kouri, a Japanese solo developer, using RPG Maker. It is short, has several endings, and belongs to a group of free Japanese horror games of the period that found large audiences online.

Its horror is mostly polite, with headless mannequins, crawling women from paintings, and walls that write messages. Its pull is in the companions Ib meets, whom the player grows to trust, and the gallery’s question of what art wants from its viewers.
