DA-0072 Film · 1978

Halloween

John Carpenter · United States

Dread 3/5

A minimal film about being watched, built from a mask and a five-note idea.

On Halloween night in Haddonfield, Illinois, a teenager named Laurie Strode notices a figure watching her from across the street. Then from behind a hedge. Then from a car.

Carpenter made the film for very little money and composed its score himself. The killer, credited as “The Shape,” wears a cheap mask and never explains himself. Donald Pleasence plays the psychiatrist who has spent years trying to understand him, and has given up.

The film’s power is the time it spends before anything happens. Most of it is watching. Laurie is watched, and so is the audience. The film lets you look at the empty space in a frame long enough to fill it yourself.
