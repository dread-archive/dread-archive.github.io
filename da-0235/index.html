DA-0235 Game · 2013

Year Walk

Simogo · Sweden

Dread 3/5

On a New Year's night in the nineteenth century, a young man walks out into the frozen forest to see his future.

A young man in rural Sweden wants to know whether the girl he loves will marry him. He goes on a årsgång, a “year walk”: an old folk ritual in which a person fasts, stays in the dark, then walks through the night to a church, to glimpse the coming year. On the way, he meets the creatures of Swedish folklore: the Huldra, the Brook Horse, and others.

Simogo, a small Swedish studio, released it first for iPhone and iPad. A companion app gave an encyclopaedia of the creatures, and contained clues needed to finish the game.

The puzzles are spare and the world is mostly snow and trees. Its dread is folk dread: rules no one explains, and creatures that are not cruel, only old and indifferent.
