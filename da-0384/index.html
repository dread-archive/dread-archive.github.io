DA-0384 Folklore · 1817

The Bell Witch

Traditional (Adams, Tennessee) · United States

Dread 3/5

America's most famous haunting, and above all a story about a voice.

Between about 1817 and 1821, according to the legend, the family of John Bell, a farmer in Robertson County, Tennessee, was tormented by an invisible presence. It began with knocking and scratching. Then it began to speak.

The spirit, later called “Kate,” talked, argued, sang and quoted sermons. It had strong opinions about the family. It was especially cruel to John Bell, whose death in 1820 the legend attributes to it. Accounts were collected and published decades later, and it is hard to separate what was reported at the time from what was added.

What makes the legend unusual is how talkative the haunting is. The Bell Witch is not a shape in the dark. It is a voice in the room, one that knows the family’s business and never shows itself.
