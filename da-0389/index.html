DA-0389 Film · 2022

Skinamarink

Kyle Edward Ball · Canada

Dread 4/5

Two small children wake at night to find their father gone, and the doors and windows disappearing.

In 1995, a four-year-old boy and his six-year-old sister wake up in the night. Their father is gone. The windows and doors of the house have vanished. They sit in the living room watching old cartoons on a tape, surrounded by darkness, while a voice from somewhere in the house begins to speak to them.

Kyle Edward Ball made the film for very little money in his childhood home. It is shot in grainy, dim images from low angles, often showing only corners, ceilings and floors. The children are rarely seen clearly.

It is not a conventional film, and many viewers find it tedious. For others it is one of the most frightening things they have seen, because it perfectly recreates a child’s fear of the house at night, and of a voice that knows your name.
