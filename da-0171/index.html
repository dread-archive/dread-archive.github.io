DA-0171 Film · 2018

Hereditary

Ari Aster · United States

Dread 5/5

A family tragedy so convincing that the horror arrives almost as a relief. It is not.

After the death of her secretive mother, Annie Graham, an artist who builds detailed miniature rooms, tries to hold her family together. Something in the house has other plans for them.

Aster’s debut is as much about grief and blame as it is about the supernatural. Toni Collette’s performance as Annie is ferocious. The film is patient, and then it is not. One moment in its first act stunned audiences into silence.

The miniatures are the key image: rooms that look real from the right angle, arranged by a hand you cannot see. By the end the whole family looks the same way.
