DA-0282 Game · 2016

Inside

Playdead; directed by Arnt Jensen · Denmark

Dread 4/5

A boy runs through a grey countryside away from men with torches and dogs, towards a facility where people are being controlled.

A boy in a red shirt runs through a wood at night, hiding from men with flashlights and vans. He crosses farms and flooded towns, passing rows of people marching in lockstep. He finds he can control some of these bodies himself, and his path leads deeper into a vast research facility.

Playdead, the Danish studio behind Limbo, spent several years making it. It has no words at all, and its animation and sound are extremely precise, with each death shown abruptly and without comment.

It never explains itself. The player is always one step from death and always going further in, and the facility and its experiments slowly become stranger. It is one of the most discussed endings in games, and best seen unprepared.
