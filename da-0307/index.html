DA-0307 Broadcast · 1992

Ghostwatch

BBC (written by Stephen Volk) · United Kingdom

Dread 5/5

A television drama that looked exactly like a live broadcast, and a nation that was not ready.

On Halloween night in 1992, BBC One aired what appeared to be a live investigation into a haunted house in suburban London, hosted by familiar presenters including Michael Parkinson. The ghost, which the family’s children called “Pipes,” was expected to make an appearance. It did.

Ghostwatch was a scripted drama, and it was listed as one. But it used real presenters, real studio sets and the rhythm of live television, and the viewers had no reason to doubt any of it. The broadcast drew a large number of complaints, and the BBC did not show it again on television for many years.

It is the clearest example in this archive of trust used as a weapon. The scariest figure in it is not the ghost. It is the calm, famous man in the studio who slowly stops being calm.
