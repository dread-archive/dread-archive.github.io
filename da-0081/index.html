DA-0081 Book · 1987

Misery

Stephen King · United States

Dread 5/5

An injured novelist is rescued by his biggest fan, and she will not let him leave until he writes what she wants.

Paul Sheldon, a successful novelist, crashes his car in a Colorado snowstorm. He wakes with shattered legs in the remote house of Annie Wilkes, a former nurse, who tells him she is his number one fan. She has just read the book in which he killed off her favourite character. She would like him to bring the character back. She has plenty of paper.

Stephen King’s novel is a two-person story set almost entirely in one room. It is about addiction, about writing, and about the uneasy relationship between a writer and the people who feel they own his work. The 1990 film adaptation won Kathy Bates an Academy Award.

The horror is not only Annie’s violence. It is that Paul keeps writing, and that the book he writes for her is good.
