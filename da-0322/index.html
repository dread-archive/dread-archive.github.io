DA-0322 Broadcast · 2015

Local 58

Kris Straub · United States

Dread 4/5

The web series that taught analog horror its grammar: plain instructions from a screen that should not be giving them.

Local 58 is a short web series presented as recordings from a small fictional UHF television station. Its episodes are what the station broadcast when something went wrong: an emergency message, a weather bulletin, a sign-off that did not sign off.

Kris Straub builds the horror out of the calm, institutional tone of old public broadcasting. Messages are polite and clear. They give instructions. The instructions become gradually impossible to follow, or impossible to want to follow.

Much of what is now called “analog horror” grew from this series. It proved that a static card and a steady voice can be more frightening than any creature.
