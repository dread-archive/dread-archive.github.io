DA-0244 Game · 2015

Bloodborne

FromSoftware (Hidetaka Miyazaki) · Japan

Dread 4/5

A hunter wakes in a gothic city during the night of the hunt. The deeper you see, the worse it gets.

A traveller comes to the city of Yharnam seeking a cure, receives a blood transfusion and wakes on the night of the hunt, when the townspeople hunt beasts in the streets. At first it seems a story of werewolves and plague. As the player gains Insight, a measure of forbidden understanding, the world changes, and vast creatures that were always there become visible.

Hidetaka Miyazaki’s game is demanding and aggressive, rewarding fast, reckless combat. Its setting moves from Victorian gothic horror to cosmic horror in the tradition of Lovecraft.

Its best idea is Insight itself: knowledge measured as a number that makes the world more dangerous as it rises. Learning more is never safe.
