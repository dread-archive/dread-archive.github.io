DA-0067 Book · 1979

Ghost Story

Peter Straub · United States

Dread 4/5

Four old men meet to tell ghost stories, and the one they never tell has come back for them.

In a small town in upstate New York, four elderly men form a club, the Chowder Society, and meet regularly to tell each other ghost stories. After one of their members dies, they begin to have the same nightmares. They send for the dead man’s nephew, a young writer who has his own story about a strange woman.

Peter Straub’s novel is long and deliberately old-fashioned, full of nods to the classic ghost stories of Henry James and Nathaniel Hawthorne. It builds slowly toward a secret from the men’s youth, fifty years before.

It is a novel about guilt that has been kept, carefully and politely, for a lifetime. The town is buried in snow, and so is the past.
