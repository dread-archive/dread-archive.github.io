DA-0395 Game · 2023

Iron Lung

David Szymanski · United States

Dread 5/5

A convict pilots a tiny submarine through an ocean of blood on a dead moon. He can only see through a camera.

The stars have gone out, and the last people alive are on space stations. A convict is welded into a small rusted submarine and lowered into an ocean of blood on a desolate moon, to photograph points of interest. If he succeeds, he will be freed. The submarine has no window. The only view outside is a black-and-white camera that takes one still photograph at a time.

David Szymanski’s short game takes about an hour to finish. The player steers using a map and coordinates, listening to the hull creak and waiting for each photograph to develop.

It is a masterpiece of limitation. The player knows only what the photographs show, and each one takes a few seconds to appear.
