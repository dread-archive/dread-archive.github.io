DA-0292 Game · 1998

Echo Night

FromSoftware · Japan

Dread 3/5

A young man follows his father's trail into a world of ghosts, who cannot rest until someone listens to them.

Richard Osmond is drawn into a mystery surrounding his father, and is carried into the past, onto a ship full of the dead. The ghosts aboard are not all hostile. Many are simply stuck, and the game is about finding what each of them needs in order to leave.

FromSoftware made it for the original PlayStation, in first person, at a time when the studio was known for King’s Field. Two sequels followed.

It is a slow, melancholy game rather than a violent one. There are no weapons. Some spirits are dangerous in the dark, and turning on lights is the only protection. What lingers is the loneliness of the dead, each one repeating its last unfinished business.
