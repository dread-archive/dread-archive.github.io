DA-0099 Film · 2002

Dark Water

Hideo Nakata · Japan

Dread 4/5

A mother and daughter move into a building where the ceiling keeps leaking from the flat above. The flat above is empty.

Yoshimi, in the middle of a divorce and a custody fight, moves with her young daughter Ikuko into a cheap flat. A dark stain spreads across the ceiling. A small red bag keeps turning up, however often she throws it away. On the roof is a water tank.

Hideo Nakata followed Ringu with another adaptation of a Koji Suzuki story. It is sadder than Ringu, and more patient. Water drips through almost every scene.

Underneath the ghost story is a story about a parent afraid of failing a child, and a child who was failed.
