DA-0403 Game · 2024

Mouthwashing

Wrong Organ · Sweden

Dread 5/5

Five crew members on a stranded freight ship in deep space, and a disaster the story keeps circling back to.

A freight ship called the Tulpar has crashed and drifted, and its five crew members are stranded in space with dwindling supplies. The captain is badly injured. The story jumps back and forth between the voyage before the disaster and the grim time after it, and the player gradually realises that the version of events they are being shown is not the whole truth.

Wrong Organ’s short game uses low-polygon graphics with a retro, uncanny feel, and relies on dialogue, atmosphere and structure rather than combat.

It is a story about guilt, power and self-deception. Its horror is in what the narrator does not want to remember, and makes the player remember for him.
