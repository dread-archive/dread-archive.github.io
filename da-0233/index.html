DA-0233 Game · 2017

Detention

Red Candle Games · Taiwan

Dread 4/5

Two students trapped in their school after a typhoon, and a ghost story about a real period of fear.

In 1960s Taiwan, under martial law, two students wake in their mountain high school to find it deserted during a typhoon. As they search for a way out, the building becomes a haunted version of itself, full of creatures drawn from Taiwanese folklore and religion.

The small studio set the game during the White Terror, a long period of political repression, when books were banned and people could be imprisoned for what they read. Slowly the story reveals a secret study group, a betrayal and a list of names.

It is a side-scrolling puzzle game with a simple look and a heavy heart. Its horror is rooted in real fear: of being watched, of informing, and of what one frightened person can do to many others.
