DA-0157 Film · 2015

The Blackcoat's Daughter

Oz Perkins · United States / Canada

Dread 4/5

Two girls are left at their Catholic boarding school over the winter break. Their parents do not come.

At the Bramford School, in the middle of winter, the term ends and the students go home. Kat and Rose are left behind: Rose because she lied to her parents about the date, and Kat because her parents have not come, and she had a bad dream about them. Elsewhere, another young woman is making her way towards the school.

Oz Perkins’s first film, originally called February, is cold, slow and precise, with long empty corridors and a heavy, droning score.

It is a film about the terrible thing loneliness might be willing to do in exchange for company.
