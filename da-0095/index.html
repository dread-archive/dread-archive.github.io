DA-0095 Film · 2007

The Mist

Frank Darabont · United States

Dread 4/5

A thick mist covers a small town, and the people trapped in the supermarket become the real threat.

After a storm, a strange mist rolls over a small town in Maine. David Drayton and his young son are in the supermarket when a bleeding man runs in, shouting that something in the mist took his friend. Soon it is clear that creatures are hunting outside. Inside, the shoppers divide into factions, and a woman preaching the end of the world begins to gather followers.

Frank Darabont adapted a Stephen King novella and changed the ending, making it much darker. King said he wished he had thought of it.

The monsters are memorable, but the film’s real subject is the supermarket: how quickly ordinary people, frightened and cut off, turn on one another.
