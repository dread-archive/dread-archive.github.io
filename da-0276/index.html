DA-0276 Game · 2012

Mad Father

Sen (miscreant's room) · Japan

Dread 3/5

An eleven-year-old girl lives in a mansion with her doctor father, who keeps a laboratory she is not allowed to enter.

Aya Drevis lives in a large house in the German countryside with her father Alfred, a doctor, and his assistant Maria. Her mother died a year ago. On the anniversary of her death, the house is overrun by the dead, and Aya goes to find her father, learning on the way what he has been doing to people in his basement.

Sen, a Japanese solo developer, made it with the free Wolf RPG Editor. It was one of a wave of small, free Japanese horror games in the early 2010s that spread through online playthroughs, and it was remade for a wider release in 2020.

Its sprites are small and cute, its descriptions often grotesque. The real horror is that Aya loves her father, and goes on loving him as she learns the truth.
