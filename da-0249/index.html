DA-0249 Game · 2016

Duskers

Misfits Attic · United States

Dread 4/5

You search derelict ships through remote drones, by typing commands. You never find out why everyone is gone.

The universe appears to be empty. The player pilots a small ship from derelict to derelict, sending remote drones inside to find fuel, scrap and logs. The drones are controlled by typing commands, one at a time, into a command line. Something is waiting in some of the rooms, and the only protection is closing the right door in time.

The game shows the ships as crude schematics, lit by sensors, and the drones’ cameras as noisy, glitching video. Its logs slowly suggest what happened to everyone, without ever quite saying it.

It is a game about isolation and about working through a keyboard, with nothing but text between you and what is in the next room.
