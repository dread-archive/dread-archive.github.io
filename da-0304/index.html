DA-0304 Broadcast · 1984

Threads

BBC (dir. Mick Jackson, written by Barry Hines) · United Kingdom

Dread 5/5

A television drama about a nuclear attack on Sheffield, and for many who saw it, the most frightening thing ever broadcast.

Ruth and Jimmy, a young couple in Sheffield, are planning their future after Ruth becomes pregnant. In the background, on radios and televisions, an international crisis is escalating. The film follows the attack and its aftermath, first in the city and then over the following years.

Written by Barry Hines and directed by Mick Jackson, Threads was made in a documentary style, with captions giving facts and figures, and was shown by the BBC in 1984. Its realism was deliberate: it was made to show what the official leaflets did not.

It is not supernatural and has no villain. The dread comes from its plainness: ordinary streets, ordinary families, and a timeline that continues long after other films would have ended.
