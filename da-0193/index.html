DA-0193 Book · 2014

Annihilation

Jeff VanderMeer · United States

Dread 4/5

An expedition into a region where nature has stopped behaving like nature, told by a narrator who is changing.

Four women, identified only by their jobs (a biologist, an anthropologist, a surveyor and a psychologist), enter Area X, a stretch of coast that has been cut off from the world for decades. Eleven expeditions have gone before them. They did not come back as themselves, when they came back at all.

The first book of VanderMeer’s Southern Reach trilogy is narrated by the biologist, in her field journal. She finds a structure going down into the ground that she insists on calling a tower, and on its walls, words written in something living.

It is less a monster story than a story about being absorbed. The biologist’s calm, observational voice makes the strangeness more disturbing, not less.
