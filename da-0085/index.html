DA-0085 Film · 1999

Audition

Takashi Miike · Japan

Dread 5/5

A lonely widower stages a fake casting call to find a new wife. For an hour, it is a gentle romance.

Seven years after his wife’s death, a television producer is encouraged by his son to remarry. A friend suggests holding auditions for a film that will never be made, as a way to meet women. He is drawn to one applicant, Asami, a quiet former ballet dancer, and begins to fall for her. Her references do not check out.

Takashi Miike adapted a novel by Ryu Murakami. For most of its running time the film is slow, sad and tender, and that is the trap. When it turns, it turns completely, and its last act is one of the most notorious sequences in horror.

It is a story about deception, told by a man who began the whole thing with a lie.
