DA-0179 Film · 2017

Verónica

Paco Plaza · Spain

Dread 4/5

During a solar eclipse in 1991, a Madrid schoolgirl uses a Ouija board to contact her dead father.

Madrid, 1991. Verónica is fifteen and looks after her three younger siblings while her widowed mother works long shifts at a bar. During a solar eclipse, she and two friends slip into the school basement with a Ouija board to reach her father. The glass breaks, Verónica faints, and in the following days the flat starts to fill with presences only she seems able to feel.

Paco Plaza, co-director of [REC], based the film loosely on a real Madrid police report from the early 1990s. A Netflix release brought it to a large international audience.

Much of its fear comes from the cramped family flat and the girl’s exhausted sense of responsibility. Whatever she has brought home, she must also protect her brother and sisters from it.
