DA-0096 Film · 1996

Scream

Wes Craven · United States

Dread 3/5

A killer targets teenagers who know all the rules of horror films. Knowing the rules does not help.

In the small town of Woodsboro, a masked killer calls his victims first and quizzes them about horror films. A year after her mother’s murder, Sidney Prescott becomes the next target. Her friends, who have seen every slasher film, try to work out the killer’s rules.

Written by Kevin Williamson and directed by Wes Craven, who had also made A Nightmare on Elm Street (DA-0076), it revived the slasher genre by making its characters as knowing as the audience. The famous opening scene kills off the actress on the poster.

It is often remembered as a comedy. It is also genuinely frightening, because it shows that knowing how a story works does not get you out of it.
