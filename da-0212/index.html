DA-0212 Game · 2001

Silent Hill 2

Konami / Team Silent · Japan

Dread 5/5

The best psychological horror game ever made, and a masterclass in making a place mean something.

James Sunderland receives a letter from his wife, Mary, asking him to meet her in their “special place” in the resort town of Silent Hill. Mary has been dead for three years. James goes anyway.

Team Silent built a fog-covered town where every monster and every building reflects the person walking through it. The creature designs by Masahiro Ito, including the figure players call Pyramid Head, are among the most analysed in games. Akira Yamaoka’s score is quiet and sad more often than loud.

The combat is deliberately clumsy. The endings depend on how you played, not on a single choice. It is a game about guilt, and about going to meet someone you know cannot be there. A full reading is in the archive’s Readings section.
