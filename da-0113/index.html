DA-0113 Film · 1998

Ringu

Hideo Nakata · Japan

Dread 5/5

A curse that spreads the way media spreads, and a final scene that crossed the screen.

A reporter, Reiko Asakawa, investigates the deaths of several teenagers. She finds a rumour about a videotape: watch it, and the phone rings. Seven days later, you die.

Nakata’s film, adapted from Koji Suzuki’s novel, started a wave of Japanese horror that the rest of the world rushed to copy. The tape itself is a short sequence of disconnected, grainy images. It should not be frightening. It is.

The genius of Ringu is its rule. The curse is not broken by being solved. It can only be survived by being copied and shown to someone else. It is a horror story about the thing every piece of media needs in order to live: someone new to watch it.
