DA-0176 Film · 2016

A Dark Song

Liam Gavin · Ireland / United Kingdom

Dread 3/5

A grieving mother rents a remote house in Wales and hires an occultist to perform a ritual that will take months.

Sophia rents a large empty house in the Welsh countryside and pays a bitter, exacting occultist named Joseph Solomon to perform the Abramelin ritual, a long ceremony of purification meant to bring the one who completes it into contact with angels. Once the salt circle is laid around the house, neither of them may leave until it is finished. She has not told him the whole truth about what she wants.

Liam Gavin’s debut has almost no cast beyond its two leads, and much of it is fasting, chanting, cold baths and arguments in bare rooms.

Its dread is procedural. The ritual is long, dull and humiliating, and every small mistake might mean starting again. When things finally begin to answer, the house has become a place nobody can walk out of.
