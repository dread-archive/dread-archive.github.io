DA-0087 Film · 1999

The Sixth Sense

M. Night Shyamalan · United States

Dread 3/5

A child psychologist tries to help a frightened boy who says he sees dead people. Built around one reveal, and earns it.

Malcolm Crowe, a child psychologist in Philadelphia, is haunted by a former patient he failed. A year later he takes on a new case: Cole, a withdrawn boy who is terrified of something he will not name. When Cole finally tells him, Malcolm must decide whether to believe him.

M. Night Shyamalan’s film was a huge hit, and famous for its ending. But it works on first viewing because it is a patient, sad film about two people who cannot make others hear them. The ghosts are frightening mainly because they are lost.

On a second viewing, every scene means something slightly different. The film was designed to be watched twice.
