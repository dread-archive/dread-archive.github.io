DA-0350 Folklore · 2009

Candle Cove

Kris Straub · United States (internet)

Dread 4/5

A short forum thread about a children's show that nobody else remembers, and one of the internet's best-known horror stories.

Written as a forum thread, Candle Cove is a conversation between adults trying to remember a children’s television show from the early 1970s. A puppet show about a girl and a pirate ship. They remember different details. The details get worse.

Kris Straub first posted the story on his website in 2009, and it spread across the internet as a creepypasta. It was later adapted for television. It is only a few hundred words long, and the ending is a single, perfect line of dialogue.

It sits in this archive under folklore because that is what it became: a story passed from person to person, which people began to half-remember as real. Nearly everyone has a memory they cannot check. This story is about what happens when you finally ask someone.
