DA-0316 Broadcast · 1977

The Southern Television broadcast interruption

Unknown · United Kingdom

Dread 3/5

A calm voice broke into the sound of an early-evening news broadcast, claiming to speak for another world.

On an evening in November 1977, viewers of the Southern Television region in the south of England heard the sound of a news broadcast replaced by a deep, distorted voice. It claimed to represent an intergalactic organisation and urged humanity to give up weapons of destruction. The picture continued as normal. After several minutes, the regular sound returned.

It was later explained as a hoax, carried out by someone who had taken over one of the region’s transmitters. The person responsible was never identified.

It is one of the best-known signal intrusions, alongside the Max Headroom incident (DA-0315). What makes it memorable is how politely it was done: a message from nowhere, interrupting an ordinary evening, and then gone.
