DA-0346 Folklore · 1942

The Vanishing Hitchhiker

Traditional (urban legend) · International

Dread 3/5

A driver gives a young woman a lift home. When he arrives, she is gone.

A driver on a lonely road at night picks up a young woman who needs a ride home. She gives an address and falls silent. When he reaches the house and turns around, the back seat is empty. At the door, an older woman explains that her daughter died years ago, on that road, and that on the anniversary of her death, drivers keep bringing her home.

Folklorists collected dozens of versions of the story in the United States in the 1940s, and it turned out to be told all over the world, with older versions involving horses and carriages.

It is one of the most widespread ghost stories there is, and one of the gentlest. The dead girl means no harm. She only wants to get home, and cannot.
