DA-0272 Game · 2006

Scratches

Nucleosys; designed by Agustín Cordes · Argentina

Dread 3/5

A horror novelist buys an isolated English manor to write in peace, and hears scratching at night.

Michael Arthate, a successful writer of horror novels, buys Blackwood Manor, a Victorian house in the English countryside, to finish his next book. Its previous owner, James Blackwood, collected African artefacts and was suspected of killing his wife. On the first night, Michael hears scratching from somewhere in the house.

Agustín Cordes, an Argentine designer, made it as a first-person point-and-click adventure with still images. It takes place over a few days, almost entirely inside the manor and its grounds.

There are almost no monsters. The fear comes from the sound, the wind and the slow discovery of documents, and from its sense that the house holds something that has not finished with its owners.
