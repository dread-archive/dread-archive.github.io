DA-0144 Film · 2013

Under the Skin

Jonathan Glazer · United Kingdom

Dread 4/5

Something wearing a woman's body drives around Glasgow, picking up men who will not be missed.

A woman with no name drives a van through the streets of Glasgow, asking men for directions and offering them a lift. Those who go home with her follow her into a dark space and do not come back. She watches people with interest and without understanding.

Jonathan Glazer loosely adapted Michel Faber’s novel. Many of the conversations were filmed with hidden cameras and men who did not know they were in a film.

It is a horror film about a mind learning to imitate a person from the outside, and discovering that imitation can change what is inside.
