DA-0217 Game · 2006

Rule of Rose

Punchline · Japan

Dread 3/5

In 1930s England, a young woman follows a boy to an orphanage, where the girls have formed a cruel aristocracy of their own.

It is 1930. Jennifer, a nineteen-year-old woman, gets off a bus in the English countryside, and a boy hands her a picture book. Following him, she reaches an old orphanage, then finds herself aboard an airship where the orphan girls rule as the Red Crayon Aristocrats. She is placed at the bottom. Her only friend is a dog called Brown.

Punchline developed it for the PlayStation 2. Its release in Europe was overshadowed by a political outcry, based on exaggerated reports of its content, and its British release was cancelled.

Combat is weak, and the game knows it. Its horror is in the hierarchy itself: little girls in dresses, humiliating each other in the name of grown-up manners, while a story of real abuse emerges between the pages.
