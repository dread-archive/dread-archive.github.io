DA-0155 Film · 2016

Train to Busan

Yeon Sang-ho · South Korea

Dread 3/5

A distracted father takes his daughter on the train from Seoul to Busan on the day the country falls.

Seok-woo, a fund manager who is always working, reluctantly takes his young daughter to see her mother in Busan. Just before the doors close, an infected woman gets on the train. The outbreak moves through the carriages faster than the train moves through the country.

Yeon Sang-ho’s film is fast, emotional and inventive in its use of narrow spaces: carriages, toilets, luggage racks and tunnels.

Its real monster is not the infected. It is the passenger who is willing to sacrifice everyone else to get to the next carriage.
