DA-0391 Game · 2022

Signalis

rose-engine · Germany

Dread 5/5

A retro survival-horror game about an android searching for someone she promised to find, and one of the best horror games of its decade.

A Replika, an android worker designated Elster, wakes up in a crashed spaceship on a frozen planet. Her partner is gone. Elster follows her trail into a vast underground facility, where the other Replikas have turned violent and the walls are papered with propaganda.

Developed by the two-person studio rose-engine, Signalis combines the fixed rooms, tight inventory and slow dread of classic survival horror with a haunting, fragmented science-fiction story. Its lo-fi look is precise and beautiful.

It is a game about memory, repetition and devotion. Elster is a copy of someone else, and she is not sure how many times she has already woken up. What she does know is that she made a promise. Everything in the game hinges on whether a copy can keep one.
