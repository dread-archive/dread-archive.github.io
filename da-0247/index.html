DA-0247 Game · 2017

Stories Untold

No Code · United Kingdom

Dread 4/5

Four short episodes played on old machines. The first is a text adventure that notices you.

The first episode is set in a 1980s bedroom. On a desk sits a home computer, and the player types commands into a text adventure called The House Abandon. The game describes a house. The player explores it. Slowly, the house on the screen and the room around the computer begin to match.

The later episodes use other old equipment, from a laboratory console to a radio receiver. Each is built around typing, tuning or following instructions, and each hides a larger story that only connects at the end.

It is a small, clever game, and its first chapter is one of the most effective pieces of horror in the medium. The terminal is not just the interface. It is where the ghost lives.
