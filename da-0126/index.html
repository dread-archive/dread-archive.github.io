DA-0126 Film · 2014

The Taking of Deborah Logan

Adam Robitel · United States

Dread 4/5

A student crew films an elderly woman with Alzheimer's disease. Her illness begins to look like something else.

A medical student and her small crew are making a documentary about Deborah Logan, a former telephone switchboard operator living with Alzheimer’s disease in Virginia. At first it is a sad, careful study of a mind failing. Then Deborah begins to speak in other voices, and to go somewhere at night.

Adam Robitel’s film uses the found-footage form to watch a person change. The performance at its centre is frightening because it starts from something real.

It is about the fear of losing yourself, and of being watched while it happens, one recording at a time.
