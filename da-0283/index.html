DA-0283 Game · 2017

Outlast 2

Red Barrels · Canada

Dread 5/5

A cameraman and his journalist wife crash in the Arizona desert, into the territory of an apocalyptic cult.

Blake Langermann and his wife Lynn, a reporter, fly into the Arizona desert to investigate the murder of a pregnant woman. Their helicopter crashes. Lynn is taken, and Blake, with only a camcorder, searches for her through a village run by a cult that believes the end of the world is near, and through a rival group that wants to stop it.

Red Barrels, a Montreal studio founded by former Ubisoft developers, followed their first Outlast with a larger, more ambitious game. The camera’s night vision drains batteries, and the player can only run, hide and climb.

It is relentless and often grotesque. At intervals, Blake finds himself back in the corridors of his old Catholic school, pursued by a memory, and these sequences make the game as much about guilt as about religion.
