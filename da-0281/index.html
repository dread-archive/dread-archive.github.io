DA-0281 Game · 2015

Fran Bow

Killmonster (Natalia Figueroa and Isaac Bauer) · Sweden

Dread 3/5

A ten-year-old girl escapes from an asylum to find her cat, and her medication shows her another world.

In 1944, Fran Bow’s parents are murdered, and she is placed in Oswald Asylum under the care of a cold doctor. All she wants is to find her black cat, Mr. Midnight. She escapes, and discovers that the red pills she is given show her a gruesome version of the world around her, which may be real.

Killmonster, a small Swedish studio of two people, made the point-and-click adventure, partly funded through crowdfunding. Its art is like a storybook, with very pale children and soft colours.

It mixes fairy-tale whimsy with very dark imagery. Its puzzles depend on switching between two views of the same place, and its unease comes from never knowing which version of Fran’s world the player should believe.
