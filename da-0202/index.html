DA-0202 Game · 2004

Yume Nikki

Kikiyama · Japan

Dread 3/5

A girl who will not leave her room explores her dreams. You save your progress by writing in her diary.

Madotsuki never leaves her apartment. Her door opens onto nothing. But when she goes to sleep, she walks through a vast world of dreams: neon deserts, endless corridors, rooms full of eyes, strange creatures that ignore her. She collects small effects that change her appearance. There is no dialogue and no explanation.

Kikiyama, the anonymous creator, released it for free, made with a simple role-playing game kit. It became a cult classic and inspired many games since.

The game is saved by having Madotsuki write in the dream diary on her desk. Everything she sees is kept there, and only there. Its ending is abrupt and has been argued over for twenty years.
