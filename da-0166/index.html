DA-0166 Film · 2015

The Witch

Robert Eggers · United States / Canada

Dread 4/5

A Puritan family alone at the edge of a forest, and a film that speaks entirely in the language of its time.

In the 1630s, a Puritan family is banished from their New England settlement and builds a farm at the edge of a vast forest. Their newborn son disappears while in the care of the eldest daughter, Thomasin. The crops fail. Suspicion turns inward.

Robert Eggers’s debut is built from period diaries and records, and much of its dialogue comes from seventeenth-century sources. It is shot in cold natural light, and it takes its characters’ faith and fears completely seriously.

The film’s horror is isolation and blame. Everyone in the family is afraid, and everyone needs someone else to be guilty. And the goat, Black Phillip, is one of the most unsettling animals in cinema.
