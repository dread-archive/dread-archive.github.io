DA-0197 Game · 1995

Clock Tower

Human Entertainment · Japan

Dread 3/5

A point-and-click horror game about hiding from a killer with giant shears, and a small classic of the genre.

Jennifer Simpson, an orphan, is adopted along with several other girls by a wealthy man and taken to his remote mansion. Soon after they arrive, the other girls disappear, and Jennifer is hunted through the house by a small figure carrying an enormous pair of scissors.

Released for the Super Famicom only in Japan, Clock Tower has no weapons and no combat. The player guides Jennifer with a pointer, searching rooms and hiding in cupboards, bathtubs and behind curtains. When the Scissorman appears, the music changes and the player has seconds to find somewhere to go.

Its sprites and menus look simple now, but its core loop, ordinary rooms that can become traps at any moment, influenced many of the hide-and-survive games that followed.
