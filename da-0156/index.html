DA-0156 Film · 2016

Under the Shadow

Babak Anvari · United Kingdom / Iran / Jordan

Dread 4/5

Tehran, 1988. As missiles fall on the city, a mother and daughter are left alone in their flat with something carried in on the wind.

During the War of the Cities, Shideh, a former medical student barred from finishing her studies, stays in Tehran with her young daughter Dorsa while her husband is sent to the front. A missile hits the building and does not explode. Afterwards, Dorsa says that a djinn has come in, and her doll goes missing.

Babak Anvari’s first feature is in Persian and was filmed in Jordan. It layers the supernatural over a real siege, and over the pressures on a woman who is being told how to live.

It is hard to tell where the djinn ends and the war begins, and the film uses that confusion carefully.
