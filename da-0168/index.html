DA-0168 Film · 2017

Get Out

Jordan Peele · United States

Dread 4/5

A sharp, funny, terrifying film about a weekend visit and the politeness that conceals what is really being asked of you.

Chris goes with his girlfriend Rose to meet her parents at their house in the countryside. They are liberal, welcoming and eager to show it. The groundskeeper and the housekeeper, both Black, are strangely stiff. The party guests are very interested in Chris.

Jordan Peele’s first film as director won the Academy Award for Best Original Screenplay. It works as satire and as pure suspense, and its central image, the Sunken Place, entered everyday language.

The horror is built from small social discomforts that grow into something much larger. The hosts never stop smiling. That is what the film asks you to be afraid of.
