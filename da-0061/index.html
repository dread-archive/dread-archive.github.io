DA-0061 Film · 1973

The Exorcist

William Friedkin · United States

Dread 4/5

Loud, grim and relentless, and still frightening because it takes itself completely seriously.

Regan MacNeil is twelve years old and lives with her mother in Georgetown. She begins to change. Doctors run test after test and find nothing. Eventually her mother turns to a priest who is losing his faith.

Friedkin adapted William Peter Blatty’s 1971 novel and filmed it like a medical procedure: cold, clinical and exhausting. Most of the running time goes to the medicine failing before anyone says the word “possession.”

It is remembered for its shocks. It works because of the mother, who watches her child being replaced by something that speaks through her, and can do nothing but keep asking for help.
