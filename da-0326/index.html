DA-0326 Broadcast · 2009

Marble Hornets

Troy Wagner & Joseph DeLage · United States

Dread 4/5

A YouTube series about a man reviewing his friend's abandoned film tapes, and the figure standing in the background of them.

Jay’s friend Alex abandoned a student film called Marble Hornets and asked Jay to destroy the tapes. Jay kept them instead. Years later, he begins going through them and uploading clips, and he notices a tall, thin figure in a suit, standing in the background of Alex’s footage.

Created in 2009 as the Slender Man legend was forming online, the series became one of the first great YouTube horror serials. It is presented entirely as found footage, with entries posted over several years, and answers left by a mysterious channel in the comments.

Its genius is the frame. Viewers watched it the way Jay does, pausing and scanning the edges of each shot, and the series rewarded that.
