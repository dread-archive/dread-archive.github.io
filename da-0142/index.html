DA-0142 Film · 2001

Session 9

Brad Anderson · United States

Dread 4/5

A small, patient film about a work crew in an abandoned asylum, and a box of old session tapes.

An asbestos removal crew takes a rushed job clearing the abandoned Danvers State Hospital in Massachusetts. They have one week. The foreman, Gordon, is exhausted and under pressure at home. One of his crew finds a box of reel-to-reel tapes: recorded therapy sessions with a former patient.

Brad Anderson shot the film in the real, derelict hospital, and the building does most of the work: endless corridors, peeling rooms, and the sense that everything in it has been waiting.

The tapes are the spine of the film. Each session reveals a little more about the patient, and about the voices she spoke in. The crew listens, one session at a time, until it matters which session they have reached.
