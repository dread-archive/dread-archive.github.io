DA-0373 Film · 2020

Saint Maud

Rose Glass · United Kingdom

Dread 4/5

A devout young nurse believes she must save the soul of the dying woman in her care.

Maud, a young private nurse who has recently converted to Catholicism, moves into a grand house in a seaside town to care for Amanda, a famous dancer dying of cancer. Maud believes God speaks to her, and that she has been given a mission: to save Amanda’s soul. Amanda finds her amusing, then less so.

Rose Glass’s debut is a small, intense film that stays close to Maud’s lonely faith and the moments of physical ecstasy that come with it. It keeps us inside her perspective, where every sign seems to confirm her calling.

It is a film about loneliness and the need to matter. Its final seconds reveal, in a single cut, the gap between how Maud sees herself and how the world does.
