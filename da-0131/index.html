DA-0131 Film · 2001

The Others

Alejandro Amenábar · Spain / United States

Dread 3/5

A quiet, candle-lit ghost story in which the rules of the household are the real source of dread.

In 1945, on the island of Jersey, Grace Stewart waits for her husband to come home from the war. She lives in a large, dim house with her two children, who are so sensitive to light that every curtain must be closed and every door locked before the next is opened. Three new servants arrive, and the house begins to fill with sounds.

Amenábar wrote, directed and scored the film, and Nicole Kidman’s performance holds it together: brittle, controlled, and certain of her rules.

It is a patient, old-fashioned ghost story with a much-discussed ending. Its best scenes are simply about moving through a house one locked door at a time.
