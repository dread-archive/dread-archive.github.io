DA-0101 Book · 1998

Uzumaki

Junji Ito · Japan

Dread 5/5

A small Japanese town becomes obsessed with spirals, and the manga makes the reader obsessed too.

In the small coastal town of Kurouzu-cho, a schoolgirl named Kirie Goshima notices that her boyfriend’s father has become fascinated with spirals: snail shells, the whorls of his fingers, the patterns in the water. His fascination grows. Soon the whole town begins to change.

Junji Ito’s manga was serialised from 1998 to 1999 and is often called his masterpiece. It is built from a series of short, escalating episodes, each one a new way for the spiral to appear. Ito’s drawing is precise and calm, which makes the transformations more disturbing.

The idea is simple and endlessly unsettling: a shape can become a curse. Once you have seen the book, you notice spirals everywhere, and you begin to wonder who noticed them first.
