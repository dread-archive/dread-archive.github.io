DA-0289 Game · 2019

The Dark Pictures Anthology: Man of Medan

Supermassive Games · United Kingdom

Dread 3/5

Four young Americans and their boat's captain go diving in the South Pacific, and end up aboard a ship that should not be there.

Two brothers, a girlfriend and a friend hire a boat captain, Fliss, for a diving holiday looking for a wartime wreck. At night, they are boarded by modern pirates and taken to a rusting cargo ship from the Second World War. Its crew are long dead, and those who walk its corridors begin to see terrible things.

It is the first of Supermassive Games’ Dark Pictures anthology, following the studio’s earlier Until Dawn. Each story is introduced by the Curator, an urbane narrator played by Pip Torrens. It loosely draws on the legend of the ghost ship Ourang Medan.

Every character can die, and choices made in panic matter. Played with friends, by passing the controller or online, it becomes a shared horror film in which each person can doom the others.
