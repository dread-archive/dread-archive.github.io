DA-0122 Film · 2010

The Last Exorcism

Daniel Stamm · United States

Dread 3/5

A preacher who has faked exorcisms for years brings a film crew to expose his last one. It is not fake.

Cotton Marcus, a charismatic Louisiana preacher, has been performing exorcisms since he was a boy, and has never believed in any of them. Troubled by stories of people harmed by the practice, he invites a documentary crew to film one last case, so he can show exactly how the trick is done. The case is a farmer’s daughter named Nell.

The film is made to look like the documentary they were shooting. Its first half is almost a comedy about a con artist with a conscience.

Then it asks the question every con artist must eventually face: what happens when the thing you have been pretending to fight turns out to be there?
