DA-0105 Film · 1987

Angel Heart

Alan Parker · United States

Dread 3/5

A private detective is hired to find a missing singer, and the trail leads somewhere he does not want to go.

New York, 1955. Harry Angel, a shabby private detective, is hired by a well-dressed stranger named Louis Cyphre to find a crooner called Johnny Favorite, who owes him a debt. The search leads south to New Orleans, and everyone Harry questions ends up dead.

Alan Parker adapted William Hjortsberg’s novel Falling Angel. The film is full of fans, lifts, dripping water and hard-boiled clichés turned sour.

It is a detective story whose client knows the answer from the start. The real question is how long it will take the detective to find himself.
