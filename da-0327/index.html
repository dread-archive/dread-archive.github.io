DA-0327 Folklore · 2010

The Russian Sleep Experiment

Unknown (creepypasta) · United States (internet)

Dread 4/5

A notorious internet story about prisoners kept awake for days in a sealed chamber.

The story claims to describe a Soviet experiment in the 1940s in which prisoners were kept in a sealed room and exposed to a gas that prevented sleep. After a few days, they stop talking to each other. Then they start whispering into the microphones. When the researchers decide to open the chamber, the prisoners do not want to leave.

Posted on a creepypasta site in 2010, it became one of the most famous stories of the genre and has been adapted many times. It is entirely fictional, although it is often shared as if it were not.

It is very violent, and it works less as a scientific horror story than as a nightmare about what might be underneath a person when everything else is stripped away.
