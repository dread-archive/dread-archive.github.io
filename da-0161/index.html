DA-0161 Film · 2016

Lights Out

David F. Sandberg · United States

Dread 3/5

A figure appears whenever the lights go out, and vanishes when they come on. It is attached to someone's mother.

Rebecca left home to get away from her mother, Sophie, who suffers from depression and talks to someone in the dark. Now her little brother Martin cannot sleep, because something visits the house whenever the light is off. It calls itself Diana.

David F. Sandberg expanded his own short film, which had spread widely online, into a feature. Its central effect is simple and very effective: the figure is only there in darkness.

It uses its ghost to talk about illness in a family, and about the children who keep the lights on for everyone else.
