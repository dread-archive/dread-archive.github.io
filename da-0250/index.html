DA-0250 Game · 2012

The Cat Lady

Harvester Games (Remigiusz Michalski) · United Kingdom / Poland

Dread 4/5

A lonely woman who has tried to end her life wakes in a strange place and is told she must hunt five people before she can die.

Susan Ashworth is a middle-aged woman living alone with her cats, isolated and severely depressed. After she takes her own life, she meets an old woman called the Queen of Maggots, who sends her back. Susan can no longer die. In return, she must find and stop five people the Queen calls Parasites. They turn out to be killers of different kinds.

Remigiusz Michalski made it with a small team, as a side-scrolling point-and-click adventure with a stark, collage-like visual style and an unusual soundtrack.

It is graphically violent, but its seriousness is what stays. The game treats depression directly and without comfort, and its horror is matched by a slow, cautious account of recovering.
