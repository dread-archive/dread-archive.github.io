The Dread Archive is a catalogue of the things people have made to frighten each other: films, books, games, broadcasts and folklore. Each entry records what the work is, how it works on you, and how it was received.

422 works are catalogued. 200 are public. Entries are rated for dread, from one to five: not how much they shock, but how long they stay.
