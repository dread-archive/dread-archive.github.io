DA-0400 Film · 2024

Late Night with the Devil

Cameron Cairnes and Colin Cairnes · Australia

Dread 4/5

A late-night talk show's 1977 Halloween episode, presented as the master tape. The guest is possessed.

The film presents itself as the recently discovered master tape of a single episode of Night Owls, a struggling American talk show, broadcast live on Halloween night in 1977. The host, desperate for ratings, has booked a psychic, a sceptic, and a parapsychologist with a teenage girl who survived a satanic cult. The girl is said to be possessed. The host would like her to demonstrate.

The Cairnes brothers recreate 1970s television in detail, with the in-studio broadcast in colour and backstage footage in black and white. David Dastmalchian gives a brilliant performance as the smooth, anxious host.

It is a film about broadcasting, ambition and the deals people make for success. Its idea, a live broadcast that went wrong and was never shown again, belongs to the archive’s favourite kind of horror.
