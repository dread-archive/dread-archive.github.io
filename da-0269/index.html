DA-0269 Game · 2008

Left 4 Dead

Turtle Rock Studios and Valve · United States

Dread 3/5

Four survivors of a sudden outbreak fight their way through a city to a rescue point, together or not at all.

Bill, a war veteran; Zoey, a student; Louis, an office worker; and Francis, a biker, are immune to a rabies-like infection that has turned the population into fast, frenzied crowds. Each campaign takes them from a safe room through streets, sewers, airports or farms to a final stand while waiting for rescue.

Turtle Rock Studios developed it with Valve as a cooperative shooter for four players. An artificial “Director” watches the players and changes the placement of enemies, items and music to keep the pressure varied.

It is often funny and chaotic, but some sounds are pure dread: the muttering of a Boomer, the roar of a Tank and, above all, the quiet crying of the Witch, sitting in the dark, who must not be disturbed. Anyone who strays from the group pays for it.
