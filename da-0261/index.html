DA-0261 Game · 2004

Obscure

Hydravision Entertainment · France

Dread 3/5

A group of students stay behind in their high school after dark to find a missing friend.

At Leafmore High, a small American school, a student called Kenny goes missing after basketball practice. His friends decide to stay in the building after hours to look for him. They find locked doors, strange plants and creatures that cannot stand light.

Hydravision, a French studio, built it around the American teen horror films of the late 1990s. Up to two players can play together, switching among five students with different skills, and characters who die stay dead.

Light is the central idea. Torches taped to weapons weaken the creatures, and breaking windows to let in the sun is a tactic. It is never as bleak as Japanese horror of the period, but the empty school at night, and the sense of a secret kept by the staff, are effective.
