DA-0069 Film · 1973

The Wicker Man

Robin Hardy · United Kingdom

Dread 4/5

Folk horror in daylight, with songs, flowers, and a community that has already decided how this ends.

Sergeant Howie, a devout police officer from the Scottish mainland, flies to the remote island of Summerisle to investigate an anonymous letter about a missing girl. The islanders are cheerful, unhelpful, and adamant that the girl never existed.

Written by Anthony Shaffer, with Christopher Lee as the island’s charming laird, the film looks more like a strange musical than a horror film for most of its length. Howie is stubborn, prudish and increasingly outraged by the island’s pagan customs, and the film does not make him easy to like.

Its reputation rests on its ending, which should not be spoiled. The horror is not a monster. It is a whole community, calm and certain, and a man who realises too late that he was invited.
