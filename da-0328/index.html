DA-0328 Folklore · 2011

NoEnd House

Brian Russell · United States (internet)

Dread 4/5

A house with nine rooms, and a prize for anyone who gets through them all.

A friend tells the narrator about a house on the edge of town that promises a cash prize to anyone who makes it from the first room to the ninth. The first rooms are like a cheap haunted attraction. Each room after that is worse, and more personal, until the narrator finds himself somewhere very familiar.

Brian Russell’s story was posted online in 2011 and became one of the best-known creepypastas. It was later adapted as a season of the television series Channel Zero (DA-0306).

Its structure is simple and effective, like a game with levels. Its last twist is the one that stays with readers, because it asks how you would know you had ever really left.
