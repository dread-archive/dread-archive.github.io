DA-0104 Film · 1975

Picnic at Hanging Rock

Peter Weir · Australia

Dread 3/5

On Valentine's Day 1900, schoolgirls on a picnic climb a volcanic rock. Some of them do not come down.

A group of girls from a boarding school in Victoria spends Valentine’s Day of 1900 at Hanging Rock. In the heat of the afternoon, several of them climb higher than they should. The watches have stopped. Three girls and a teacher vanish.

Peter Weir adapted Joan Lindsay’s 1967 novel into a film of heat haze, pan pipes and white dresses. Nothing violent is shown, and nothing is resolved.

It is one of the purest examples of dread without a monster. The rock simply keeps what it took, and gives no reasons.
