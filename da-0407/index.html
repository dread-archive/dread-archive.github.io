DA-0407 Film · 2025

28 Years Later

Danny Boyle and Alex Garland · United Kingdom

Dread 4/5

Decades after the Rage virus, a boy from an island community makes his first trip to the mainland.

Nearly three decades after the outbreak of the Rage virus, Britain is quarantined from the world. A small community survives on a tidal island, connected to the mainland by a causeway only at low tide. Twelve-year-old Spike is taken by his father on his first trip across to hunt the infected, as a rite of passage. Later he returns, on his own mission, with his sick mother.

Danny Boyle and Alex Garland returned to the world they created in 28 Days Later. The film mixes intense horror with strange beauty, English folklore and grief, and introduces new kinds of infected.

Its most memorable image is a temple built of bones, made by a doctor who has spent years honouring the dead, and its tone is closer to a fairy tale than a war film.
