DA-0170 Film · 2017

Ghost Stories

Jeremy Dyson and Andy Nyman · United Kingdom

Dread 4/5

A professor who debunks psychics is sent three cases that he cannot explain away.

Professor Phillip Goodman makes television programmes exposing fake mediums. His childhood hero, a famous investigator who vanished years ago, sends him three files: a night watchman, a teenager who hit something in the woods, and a wealthy man awaiting the birth of his child. Each seems unexplainable.

Jeremy Dyson and Andy Nyman adapted their own stage play, which had been a hit in London for its jump scares and its insistence that audiences keep its secrets.

It is a portmanteau film with a structure that means more than it seems, about a man who has built a career on not being fooled.
