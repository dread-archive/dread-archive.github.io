DA-0223 Game · 2004

The Suffering

Surreal Software · United States

Dread 4/5

A death-row prisoner arrives at an island penitentiary on the day the creatures come out.

Torque has been sentenced to death for the murder of his family, which he says he does not remember. He arrives at Abbott State Penitentiary on Carnate Island, off the coast of Maryland, just as an earthquake shakes the prison and monstrous creatures pour out of the ground. Escaping through the prison and the island, he begins to have visions of what happened that day.

Surreal Software built it for Midway. The creatures are designed after methods of execution and killing, drawing on the island’s history of hangings, gas chambers and massacres.

The game lets the player’s choices shape what kind of man Torque turns out to be. That uncertainty about his own guilt follows him through every corridor.
