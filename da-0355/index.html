DA-0355 Folklore · 2008

Smile Dog

Anonymous (internet) · Internet

Dread 3/5

An early internet legend about an image that haunts whoever sees it, until they show it to someone else.

The story describes a photograph, usually called smile.jpg: a dog, in a dark room, lit by a camera flash, with a wide and entirely human grin. People who see it are said to be haunted by it in their dreams, and told that the only relief is to “spread the word.”

It circulated as a creepypasta in the late 2000s and became one of the best-known early examples of the form. Many versions exist, and many edited images claim to be the original.

Like Ringu, it is a story about a curse that can only be survived by passing it on. It is not a coincidence that the internet found this idea so easy to share.
