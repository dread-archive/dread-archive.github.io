DA-0094 Film · 1982

The Thing

John Carpenter · United States

Dread 5/5

The best film ever made about trust, disguised as a monster movie.

At a research station in Antarctica, twelve men take in a dog that was being chased across the ice. The dog is not a dog. Whatever it is can copy anything it touches, perfectly.

Carpenter’s film is based on John W. Campbell’s 1938 novella Who Goes There? It was poorly received on release and has been reappraised ever since. The practical creature effects are still astonishing, but they are not the point. The point is the room full of men who can no longer be sure about each other, or about themselves.

The blood-test scene is one of the great sequences in horror: a simple procedure, a line of suspects, and a hot wire. The ending refuses to tell you who is still human. It trusts you to be uncomfortable with that.
