DA-0268 Game · 2008

Siren: Blood Curse

Japan Studio; directed by Keiichiro Toyama · Japan

Dread 4/5

An American television crew investigating a legend in a remote Japanese mountain village arrive on the night of a forbidden ritual.

A small American crew travel to Japan to film a programme about Hanuda, a mountain village said to have disappeared after a human sacrifice. They witness a ritual, a siren sounds, and the sea around the village turns red. The villagers are now Shibito, the walking dead, and they still go about their routines.

Keiichiro Toyama, who also directed the first Silent Hill, returned to reimagine his 2003 Siren for the PlayStation 3. It was first released in episodes through the PlayStation Network.

As before, the player can “sightjack”, seeing through the eyes of nearby Shibito to learn where they are walking. Watching yourself from a monster’s point of view, as it walks towards your hiding place, is the series’ most unsettling idea.
