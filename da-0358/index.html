DA-0358 Broadcast · 2012

Welcome to Night Vale

Joseph Fink & Jeffrey Cranor · United States

Dread 2/5

A podcast presented as a small desert town's community radio, where the weather is a song and the sinister is routine.

Welcome to Night Vale is a twice-monthly podcast presented as the community radio broadcast of a small desert town. Its host, Cecil, reads the news in a warm and friendly voice: the hooded figures in the dog park, the mysterious lights above the Arby’s, and the city council’s latest unexplained decree.

Created by Joseph Fink and Jeffrey Cranor and voiced by Cecil Baldwin, it began in 2012 and became one of the first breakout fiction podcasts. It is mostly funny, and it is often tender.

It belongs in this archive for its method. It uses the calm voice of local broadcasting to deliver the impossible, and it shows how a friendly, reassuring tone can make almost anything sound normal.
