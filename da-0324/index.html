DA-0324 Folklore · 2004

The Dionaea House

Eric Heisserer · United States (internet)

Dread 4/5

A story told through emails and blog posts about a house that always looks slightly different.

A man tries to understand an old friend’s sudden, violent crime, through the emails and letters the friend left behind. They describe a house. There are other accounts online, on different blogs, written by people who did not know each other, describing a house with the same wrong details, and what happened to people who went inside.

Eric Heisserer, who later became a Hollywood screenwriter, told the story in 2004 through a network of fake websites and emails, so that readers found it as scattered documents rather than a single text.

It was one of the first horror stories to use the internet as its form, not just as a place to post. The story is the archive, and the reader has to assemble it.
