DA-0158 Film · 2014

Creep

Patrick Brice · United States

Dread 3/5

A videographer answers an online ad to film a man for a day. The man is very friendly.

Aaron, a freelance videographer, drives to a remote cabin for a one-day job. The client, Josef, says he is dying and wants to record a video diary for his unborn son. Josef is warm, strange, and full of surprises, and he does not want Aaron to leave.

Patrick Brice made the film with Mark Duplass, who plays Josef, with only the two of them on screen. It is mostly improvised.

It is the found-footage film turned around: the camera is not there to catch the monster. The monster hired it.
