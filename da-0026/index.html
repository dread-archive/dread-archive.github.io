DA-0026 Film · 1960

Eyes Without a Face

Georges Franju · France / Italy

Dread 4/5

A surgeon's crimes, filmed like a fairy tale. Cold, beautiful and quietly devastating.

A celebrated surgeon’s daughter, Christiane, has been disfigured in a car accident he caused. She lives hidden in his house outside Paris, wearing a smooth white mask. Her father, with the help of his devoted assistant, lures young women to the house and removes their faces, trying to graft a new one onto his daughter.

Georges Franju films this with remarkable restraint. The notorious surgery scene is shown in steady, clinical detail, and audiences fainted at early screenings. The rest of the film is quiet: long corridors, barking dogs in the basement, Christiane drifting through rooms in her mask.

Christiane is the centre of the film and its most haunting image: a young woman whose face is her father’s project and not her own. The ending gives her a choice, and she takes it.
