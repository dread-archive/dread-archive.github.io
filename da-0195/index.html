DA-0195 Game · 2005

Haunting Ground

Capcom · Japan

Dread 4/5

A young woman wakes in a castle, pursued by its residents. Her only ally is a dog.

After a car accident, Fiona Belli wakes up in a cage inside a strange castle, wrapped in a sheet. She cannot fight well. The castle’s inhabitants pursue her one after another, each wanting something from her. She finds a white German shepherd named Hewie, and learning to trust and command him is the heart of the game.

Capcom’s game is a successor in spirit to the Clock Tower series (DA-0197). It relies on hiding, running and a panic system in which Fiona’s fear affects the screen and her control.

It was not a big seller, and for years it was hard to find, which added to its reputation. Its most lasting achievement is the bond between the player, Fiona and the dog.
