DA-0185 Book · 1983

The Woman in Black

Susan Hill · United Kingdom

Dread 4/5

A young solicitor travels to a remote town for a client's funeral, and notices a woman in black standing in the churchyard.

Arthur Kipps, a junior solicitor, is sent from London to Crythin Gifford to attend the funeral of Mrs Alice Drablow and sort through her papers. Her home, Eel Marsh House, stands on the marshes and can only be reached by a causeway that disappears at high tide. At the funeral he sees a woman with a wasted face whom nobody else will discuss. At the house, in the fog, he hears a pony and trap.

Susan Hill wrote it as a deliberate classical English ghost story. Stephen Mallatratt’s stage version has been running in London’s West End since 1989.

Kipps narrates long afterwards, on a Christmas Eve when his family are telling ghost stories. He will not tell one aloud, and that refusal is the first frightening thing in the book.
