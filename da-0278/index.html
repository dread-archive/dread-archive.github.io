DA-0278 Game · 2013

Cry of Fear

Team Psykskallar · Sweden

Dread 4/5

A depressed young man is hit by a car and wakes in a deserted Scandinavian city at night, following a trail of messages.

Simon Henriksson, a nineteen-year-old, sees a man lying injured on the pavement and goes to help. A car hits him. He wakes in an alley in an empty, rain-soaked city full of disfigured creatures, and follows notes and voices through apartments, trains and a hospital, towards someone who seems to know him.

Team Psykskallar, a small Swedish team, made it as a free game on the engine of Half-Life, after beginning it as a modification. It was released on Steam for free in 2013 and attracted a large audience.

It is crude in places, and its combat is tense and unforgiving, with weapons that wobble in Simon’s hands. Its real subject is depression and self-harm, and the city, with its locked doors and pursuers, is a portrait of a mind closing in on itself.
