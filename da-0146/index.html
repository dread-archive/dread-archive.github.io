DA-0146 Film · 2007

The Orphanage

J. A. Bayona · Spain

Dread 4/5

A ghost story about a mother, a missing child and an old seaside house, as moving as it is frightening.

Laura returns with her husband and adopted son, Simón, to the seaside orphanage where she grew up, planning to reopen it as a home for children with disabilities. Simón starts talking about new invisible friends. During a party, he disappears. Laura glimpses a child wearing a sack mask.

J. A. Bayona’s first feature, produced by Guillermo del Toro, balances real grief with classic haunted-house suspense. Belén Rueda carries the film as a mother who refuses to stop searching, whatever it costs.

A children’s game, one-two-three, knock on the wall, becomes the film’s most frightening sequence. Like the best ghost stories, it is ultimately about loss, and about a door a parent keeps open long after everyone else has closed it.
