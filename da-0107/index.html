DA-0107 Film · 1987

Prince of Darkness

John Carpenter · United States

Dread 4/5

Physicists study a canister of green liquid under a church. Each night they share the same dream: a message sent from the future.

A priest invites a physics professor and his graduate students to examine an ancient canister in the basement of a Los Angeles church. The liquid inside seems to be aware. Outside, the homeless people of the neighbourhood gather and stand still, watching.

John Carpenter’s film mixes theology and quantum physics with a siege. Its most frightening element is a grainy video the researchers all see in their sleep: a broadcast from the future, describing what is about to happen, as a warning.

It is a film about a message that arrives the wrong way through time, received by people who have no way to answer it.
