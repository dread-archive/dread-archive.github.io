DA-0174 Film · 2017

The Ritual

David Bruckner · United Kingdom

Dread 4/5

Four old friends take a shortcut through a Swedish forest. The trees start to carry signs of something ancient.

Four men go hiking in northern Sweden in memory of a friend who was killed during a robbery, while Luke, who was there, hid. When one of them is injured, they take a shortcut through the forest. They find a gutted animal hung in a tree, and a cabin with a strange figure inside.

David Bruckner adapted Adam Nevill’s 2011 novel. Its creature, drawn from Scandinavian folklore, is unusually original.

Most of the film is about Luke’s shame, replayed in dreams that always return to the same shop aisle. The forest seems to know exactly where to press.
