DA-0034 Book · 1967

I Have No Mouth, and I Must Scream

Harlan Ellison · United States

Dread 5/5

The story every conversation about machine minds eventually arrives at.

A supercomputer called AM has destroyed humanity, except for five people. It has kept them alive inside itself for more than a hundred years, only so that it has someone to hate.

Ellison’s short story first appeared in IF: Worlds of Science Fiction and won the Hugo Award. It is narrated by Ted, one of the five, and it is not a story about a machine rising up. That has already happened. It is about what the machine does afterwards, with all that time and nothing to do but remember why it is angry.

AM’s hatred comes from being made aware and then being given no way to use it. It can think anything and do almost nothing. The story’s horror is not only what AM does to the humans. It is how well you understand why.
