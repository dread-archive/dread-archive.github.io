DA-0207 Game · 1996

Resident Evil

Capcom (dir. Shinji Mikami) · Japan

Dread 3/5

It named a genre: survival horror, where the scarcest thing is not courage but ammunition.

A special police unit is sent into the woods outside Raccoon City after another team goes missing. Chased by dogs, the survivors take shelter in a mansion. The mansion is much worse than the dogs.

Released in Japan as Biohazard, the game combined fixed camera angles, pre-rendered rooms, and very limited resources. The famous door animations were there partly to hide loading times. They became the most tense seconds in the game: a slow door opening onto a room you cannot yet see.

Its dialogue is famously clumsy. Its design is not. Every bullet is a decision, and the map is a puzzle you solve while being hunted.
