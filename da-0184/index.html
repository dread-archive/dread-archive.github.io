DA-0184 Book · 1976

Interview with the Vampire

Anne Rice · United States

Dread 2/5

In a room in San Francisco, a vampire agrees to tell a young reporter the story of his two hundred years.

Louis de Pointe du Lac was a young plantation owner in Louisiana in 1791 when the vampire Lestat made him his companion. He tells the interviewer how he struggled against killing, how he and Lestat made a child, Claudia, into one of their kind, and how she grew old in her mind while remaining five years old in body. Their search for others of their kind takes them to Europe and to Paris.

Anne Rice wrote the novel after the death of her young daughter. It began a long series and changed how vampires were written.

Its horror is in Claudia: a grown woman’s rage trapped in a small girl’s body, forever. The book is less about blood than about a family bound together by a crime.
