DA-0404 Film · 2024

Nosferatu (2024)

Robert Eggers · United States

Dread 4/5

A new, lavish telling of the 1922 classic, centred on the woman the vampire is drawn to.

In 1838, a young estate agent travels to the Carpathian Mountains to sell a house to the reclusive Count Orlok. Back in Germany, his wife, Ellen, suffers from visions and fits. Orlok is coming, and he is coming for her. In this version, Ellen has summoned something long before she met her husband.

Robert Eggers remade F. W. Murnau’s silent film (DA-0001), which he has said he loved since childhood, with exhaustive period detail and deep, candlelit darkness. Bill Skarsgård’s Orlok is a rotting nobleman with a huge, rumbling voice.

It is less a copy than an argument with the original, putting Ellen’s desire and loneliness at the centre of the story. The archive has catalogued both versions. It will not say which it prefers.
