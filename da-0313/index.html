DA-0313 Broadcast · 1979

Sapphire & Steel

P. J. Hammond (ATV) · United Kingdom

Dread 4/5

Two mysterious agents are sent wherever time has broken in. A science fiction series that played like a ghost story.

Sapphire and Steel look human but are not. They are sent to places where time has broken through into the present: a farmhouse where nursery rhymes make people vanish, an abandoned railway station haunted by the dead of old wars, a flat above a photography shop where the past is trapped in pictures. Their job is to seal the breaks, whatever it costs the people involved.

Written by P. J. Hammond and starring Joanna Lumley and David McCallum, the series was made cheaply in the studio, with minimal sets, and it did not explain itself.

Its stories are slow, cold and often frightening, and its last episode ends with the two agents trapped in a place with no way out. It was never resolved.
