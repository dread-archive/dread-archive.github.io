DA-0151 Film · 2008

Lake Mungo

Joel Anderson · Australia

Dread 5/5

A mock documentary about a family's grief that becomes one of the most quietly frightening films of its decade.

After sixteen-year-old Alice Palmer drowns during a family outing, her parents and brother begin to notice something in the photographs and video they take around the house. A figure, standing in the background.

Joel Anderson made the film as a documentary, with interviews, home video and still photographs, and plays it almost entirely straight. There are no jump scares in the usual sense. There are images that make you stop the film and look again.

It is mostly a story about grief, and about how little a family knows about each other. The most frightening moment in it has been paused and discussed endlessly since. It is a single image, and it is not what you expect.
