DA-0305 Broadcast · 1990

Twin Peaks

David Lynch and Mark Frost · United States

Dread 4/5

A murder mystery in a small logging town that opens onto something much darker than murder.

The body of Laura Palmer, the town’s homecoming queen, is found wrapped in plastic on a riverbank. FBI Special Agent Dale Cooper arrives to investigate, delighting in the local coffee and cherry pie. The town is full of secrets, and some of them are not human.

David Lynch and Mark Frost’s series mixed soap opera, comedy and small-town melodrama with sequences of pure nightmare: a red room where people speak backwards, a figure glimpsed behind a bed, a grieving mother’s scream.

It changed what television could be, and the horror at its centre has lost none of its power. The question of who killed Laura Palmer turned out to be less frightening than the answer.
