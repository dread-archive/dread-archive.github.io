DA-0128 Film · 2011

The Innkeepers

Ti West · United States

Dread 3/5

Two bored clerks spend the last weekend of an old hotel trying to record its ghost.

The Yankee Pedlar Inn is closing for good. Its last two employees, Claire and Luke, use the final nights to hunt for the ghost of Madeline O’Malley, a bride said to have died there. Claire walks the empty corridors with headphones and an audio recorder, listening for voices in the hiss.

Ti West takes his time. For most of the film, very little happens, and it happens slowly and in silence.

The scenes of Claire listening are the heart of it: the hope that a recording will contain something, and the fear that it will.
