DA-0198 Game · 1995

Phantasmagoria

Roberta Williams (Sierra On-Line) · United States

Dread 3/5

A writer and her husband buy a mansion once owned by a stage magician. He was a collector of things he should not have called up.

Adrienne Delaney, a novelist, moves with her husband Don into an old house on a New England island. It once belonged to Zoltan Carnovasch, a nineteenth-century magician called Carno, whose wives all died. Exploring the house, Adrienne opens a hidden chapel and lets out something that settles in Don. He grows cold and then violent.

Roberta Williams, co-founder of Sierra and creator of King’s Quest, made it as a full-motion video adventure. It filled seven CDs and featured actors filmed on blue screen. Its graphic death scenes and a scene of sexual assault made it controversial.

The film-like death scenes date badly. Its more lasting fear is domestic: a woman watching her husband turn into a stranger in a house she chose.
