DA-0063 Film · 1975

Jaws

Steven Spielberg · United States

Dread 3/5

A shark terrorises a beach town, and a whole generation became uneasy about open water.

On Amity Island, a summer resort town, a young woman is killed by a shark at the start of the summer season. The new police chief wants to close the beaches. The mayor refuses. More people die. The chief, a marine biologist and a hardened shark hunter go out to sea to kill it.

Steven Spielberg adapted Peter Benchley’s novel, and the production was famously troubled: the mechanical shark kept breaking down, so for much of the film it could not be shown. Spielberg suggested it instead with a yellow barrel, a point-of-view camera, and John Williams’s two-note theme.

It became the first modern summer blockbuster. Its lasting achievement is simpler: it made the surface of the sea into a threat, for anyone who has ever looked down and not been able to see their feet.
