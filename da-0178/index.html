DA-0178 Film · 2017

Tigers Are Not Afraid

Issa López · Mexico

Dread 3/5

A girl whose mother has vanished is given three wishes in the form of chalk, in a city emptied by cartel violence.

During a shootout, Estrella’s teacher gives her three pieces of chalk and tells her they are three wishes. When she gets home, her mother is gone, one of the many people who have disappeared from the town. Estrella joins a small gang of orphaned boys led by Shine, who has stolen a gun and a phone from a cartel member. A thin line of blood begins to follow her through the streets.

Issa López’s film, Vuelven in Spanish, was widely compared to the work of Guillermo del Toro, who later championed it.

Its ghosts are real enough, but the film never lets them become the main threat. The adults with guns are far worse, and the children’s fairy tales are the only defences they have.
