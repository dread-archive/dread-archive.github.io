DA-0308 Broadcast · 1934

Lights Out

Wyllis Cooper, later Arch Oboler (NBC) · United States

Dread 3/5

A late-night radio horror series that told listeners to turn off the lights before it began.

Broadcast late at night, Lights Out was one of the first radio series devoted entirely to horror. Created by Wyllis Cooper and later written by Arch Oboler, it told listeners to turn out their lights, and then used sound effects, whispered narration and stream-of-consciousness monologues to frighten them in the dark.

Its episodes were known for their gruesome sound effects, made with vegetables, bacon fat and other kitchen tricks, and for their inventive stories, including one about a chicken heart that grows until it swallows the world.

Many episodes are lost. The ones that survive show how much horror radio could do with nothing but voices and noise, and how much the listener’s own imagination did the rest.
