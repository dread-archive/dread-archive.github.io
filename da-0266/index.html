DA-0266 Game · 2005

Fatal Frame III: The Tormented

Tecmo · Japan

Dread 4/5

A photographer who survived the car crash that killed her fiancé dreams each night of a house where it is always snowing.

Rei Kurosawa, a photographer, was driving the car in which her fiancé Yuu died. While photographing an old house, she sees him. That night she begins dreaming of the Manor of Sleep, a vast Japanese house under constant snow, where a woman covered in tattoos is waiting. Each morning the tattoo-like marks on Rei’s own body have spread.

Tecmo’s third game in the series, known as Project Zero 3 in Europe, brings back the Camera Obscura, which damages ghosts when they are photographed. It also adds two playable characters linked to earlier games.

It is about survivor’s guilt, and about the temptation to stay asleep with the dead. The house is beautiful and quiet, and the player returns to it every night, knowing it is a little more dangerous each time.
