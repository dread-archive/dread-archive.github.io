DA-0145 Film · 2012

Resolution

Justin Benson and Aaron Moorhead · United States

Dread 3/5

A man handcuffs his addicted friend in a cabin for a week, to get him clean. Someone keeps leaving them recordings.

Michael drives out to a remote cabin, where his old friend Chris is living on drugs, and chains him to a pipe for a week of forced withdrawal. While Chris rages, Michael starts finding things: photographs, a slide projector, old tapes and films, each showing some other story that ended badly.

Justin Benson and Aaron Moorhead made it on a tiny budget, and it is as much a film about a friendship as a horror film. It later connects to their film The Endless.

Its unsettling idea is an unseen audience, collecting stories and waiting to see how this one ends.
