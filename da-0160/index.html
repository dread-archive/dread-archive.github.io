DA-0160 Film · 2016

Personal Shopper

Olivier Assayas · France

Dread 3/5

An American in Paris waits for a sign from her dead twin brother. Then her phone begins to receive texts from an unknown sender.

Maureen buys clothes for a famous, demanding client. She is also a medium, like her twin brother Lewis, who died recently of a heart condition they shared. They promised that whoever died first would send the other a sign. She spends nights in his empty house, waiting.

Olivier Assayas’s film is part ghost story, part thriller. Its most famous sequence is a long exchange of text messages with an unknown number that seems to know where she is.

It never settles the question of who is on the other side of the screen: the dead, the living, or only her own wish for someone to be there.
