DA-0066 Film · 1978

Dawn of the Dead

George A. Romero · United States / Italy

Dread 4/5

Four survivors of a zombie outbreak hide in a shopping mall, and discover they like it there.

A decade after Night of the Living Dead (DA-0055), George A. Romero returned to the end of the world. Four people escape a collapsing city by helicopter and land on the roof of a suburban shopping mall. They clear it of the dead, lock the doors and settle in, surrounded by everything money can buy. Outside, the dead keep coming to the mall, out of habit.

It is a horror film and a sharp satire of consumerism at the same time, and it is often very funny. Tom Savini’s make-up effects set a new standard for gore, but the lingering images are the shoppers who no longer know why they are there.

The survivors are safe, comfortable and slowly dying of boredom. That is the film’s real warning.
