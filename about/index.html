About the archive

The Dread Archive is a private catalogue, kept since 2015, of works made to frighten: films, books, games, broadcasts and folklore. It is not a review site, and it does not accept submissions.

Each entry has a catalogue number, a short description, and a dread rating from one to five. Dread measures how long a work stays with you, not how much it shocks.

Below each description is a line. The fields below the line are kept for the archive's own purposes, and have been recorded since 2023. They are published as they are and are not explained. The catalogue date is the date a work entered the archive. Entries are made public later, in batches, once they have been reviewed.

Since 2023, entries are written with the help of an assistant. Every entry is read by a human before it is made public.

Corrections are not accepted.

Preserving the dark. Some things shouldn't be forgotten.
