DA-0333 Broadcast · 2014

Too Many Cooks

Casper Kelly (Adult Swim) · United States

Dread 3/5

An eleven-minute sitcom intro that loops, grows and slowly turns into something else, first aired unannounced in the small hours.

It begins as a parody of a cheerful 1980s family sitcom opening: a catchy theme song, smiling actors turning to the camera, their names appearing on screen. Then more characters appear. And more. The credits sequence refuses to end, drifting through other genres, until a figure with a knife begins to appear in the background.

Created by Casper Kelly, it first aired on Adult Swim in 2014 in an early-morning slot usually used for infomercials, with no announcement, before spreading online.

It is funny, and it is genuinely unnerving. It shows how quickly familiar broadcast formats can become frightening when they refuse to behave, and how strange it feels to stumble on something at 4 a.m. that no one told you would be there.
