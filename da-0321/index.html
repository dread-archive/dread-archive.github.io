DA-0321 Broadcast · 2016

This House Has People in It

Alan Resnick (Adult Swim) · United States

Dread 3/5

Footage from a home security system watching an ordinary family, whose daughter is lying face-down on the floor.

The video appears to be footage from a home security company’s cameras, switching between rooms of a suburban house. The family goes about its evening. Their teenage daughter lies face-down on the floor, and nobody seems concerned. Something in the house is wrong, and the cameras keep recording it.

Created by Alan Resnick for Adult Swim, it came with a website and hidden material that viewers pieced together into a larger story about the family and their house.

It is quiet, almost boring, which is exactly what makes it frightening. The cameras see everything, and understand nothing.
