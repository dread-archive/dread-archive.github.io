DA-0172 Film · 2017

The Endless

Justin Benson and Aaron Moorhead · United States

Dread 3/5

Two brothers who escaped a cult return for one visit. Time behaves strangely around the camp.

Justin and Aaron Smith left a group called Camp Arcadia as young men and told the world it was a UFO death cult. Years later, broke and unhappy, they receive a videotape from the camp and go back for a single night. The members have not aged, and there are two moons in the sky.

Benson and Moorhead play the brothers themselves. The film shares its world with their earlier Resolution, and shows what became of its characters.

Around the camp, many people are trapped in loops of different lengths, living the same stretch of time again and again. Some know it. Some have accepted it.
