DA-0245 Game · 2016

Pony Island

Daniel Mullins Games · United States

Dread 3/5

A cheerful arcade game about a jumping pony, made by the Devil, and you are trapped inside it.

The player is sitting at an arcade machine with a pony game on it. The game is broken. Someone else is trapped inside it, leaving messages for the player. The machine’s owner, who turns out to be Lucifer, would very much like the player’s soul, and the only way out is to hack the game from the inside.

Daniel Mullins went on to make Inscryption (DA-0371), and many of its ideas begin here: a game that pretends to break, messages from others who were trapped before you, and a menu screen that is not safe.

It is short and very funny, and its horror comes from its playfulness. The Devil is not frightening. What is frightening is how willing you are to keep playing.
