DA-0183 Book · 1962

Something Wicked This Way Comes

Ray Bradbury · United States

Dread 2/5

A carnival arrives in a small town at three in the morning in late October. Two boys sneak out to watch it set up.

Will Halloway and Jim Nightshade are almost fourteen and live next door to each other in Green Town, Illinois. A lightning-rod salesman warns of a storm. Then, in the small hours, Cooger & Dark’s Pandemonium Shadow Show arrives by train, and the boys watch its tents rise without a sound. Its carousel can run backwards, and its owner, Mr. Dark, is covered in tattoos that move.

Ray Bradbury developed the novel from an earlier short story and a screen idea. It is one of his best-loved books, and one of the few in which Will’s ageing father, a library janitor, becomes the hero.

It frightens through temptation. The carnival preys on what people wish for, youth or age, and the boys must decide whether to want what they are offered.
