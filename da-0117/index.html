DA-0117 Film · 2004

Premonition

Norio Tsuruta · Japan

Dread 3/5

A teacher finds a scrap of newspaper announcing his daughter's death, minutes before it happens.

Hideki is waiting in his car by a phone box when he notices a torn newspaper page describing a fatal accident, with a photograph of his young daughter. Before he can act, it happens. Years later he goes looking for other people who have received the “terror newspaper”.

The film was part of a series of J-horror productions released together. It is based on a manga by Jirō Tsunoda.

It is less about the ghost than about the guilt of having been warned, and the question of whether anyone can use a warning in time.
