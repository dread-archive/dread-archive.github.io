DA-0312 Broadcast · 1971

A Ghost Story for Christmas

Lawrence Gordon Clark (BBC) · United Kingdom

Dread 4/5

An annual series of short ghost story films broadcast by the BBC at Christmas, most of them from M. R. James.

Beginning in 1971, the BBC broadcast a new ghost story film each Christmas, most of them directed by Lawrence Gordon Clark and adapted from the stories of M. R. James (DA-0014). They include tales of a stolen crown buried on the coast, a treasure guarded in a well, and an ash tree outside a manor that holds a secret.

The films were shot on location, on film, with small casts and very little shown. Their atmosphere of damp churches, empty beaches and grey English light is now considered classic.

The tradition lapsed and was revived several times. For many British viewers, these films defined what a ghost story looks like: quiet, scholarly, and ending just after you see something you cannot explain.
