DA-0188 Book · 2000

House of Leaves

Mark Z. Danielewski · United States

Dread 4/5

A book that becomes the house it describes. You have to turn it in your hands to read it.

A family moves into a house on Ash Tree Lane and discovers that it measures a quarter of an inch larger on the inside than on the outside. Then a door appears where there was none, opening onto a dark, cold hallway that keeps going.

The story of the house is told through The Navidson Record, a documentary that may not exist, described in an academic study by a blind old man named Zampanò, which is annotated by a young man named Johnny Truant, who is not coping.

Pages run sideways and backwards. Footnotes swallow the text. Some sections are missing or burned. It sounds like a gimmick. In practice it makes the reader do what the characters do: go further in than is sensible, and lose track of the way back.
