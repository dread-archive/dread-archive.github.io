DA-0264 Game · 2001

White Day: A Labyrinth Named School

Sonnori · South Korea

Dread 4/5

A student sneaks into his school at night to leave a present for a girl, and the doors lock behind him.

Hee-min Lee breaks into his high school late at night to leave a gift on the desk of a girl he likes, before White Day. The gates lock. Other students are trapped inside, the corridors are patrolled by a violent janitor, and ghosts move through the classrooms, toilets and stairwells.

Sonnori made it for Korean PCs, with ghosts taken from the country’s school ghost stories. It was little known abroad until a remake in the mid-2010s brought it to consoles and to a wider audience.

The player cannot fight. They hide in toilet cubicles, listen for the janitor’s keys and footsteps, and learn the school by the sound of each room. Its ghosts are memorable, but it is the routine of listening and hiding that makes it frightening.
