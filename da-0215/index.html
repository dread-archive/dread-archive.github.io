DA-0215 Game · 2005

Condemned: Criminal Origins

Monolith Productions · United States

Dread 4/5

An FBI investigator, framed for murder, chases a serial killer through the city's abandoned buildings, armed with whatever he can pick up.

Ethan Thomas, an agent with an FBI unit that hunts serial killers, is framed for the deaths of two police officers at a crime scene. Fleeing, he follows the trail of a killer who seems to be stalking other killers. The city is in the grip of strange violence, and its derelict shops, schools and subways are full of people who attack without reason.

Monolith made it as a launch title for the Xbox 360. It is played in first person, mostly with improvised melee weapons: pipes, planks, locker doors. Forensic tools are used to read crime scenes.

The fights are close and clumsy. Attackers hide behind mannequins and in shadows, and the sound of a pipe striking a wall carries a long way through a dead building.
