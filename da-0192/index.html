DA-0192 Game · 1998

Parasite Eve

Square · Japan

Dread 3/5

A police officer at the opera watches the audience burst into flames. A cinematic sequel to a Japanese horror novel.

On Christmas Eve in New York, a rookie police officer named Aya Brea attends the opera. During the performance, the lead singer causes almost everyone in the audience to catch fire. Aya is unaffected. Over the next six days she pursues the singer, now calling herself Eve, across the city, as the creature tries to take control of all life through the mitochondria inside every cell.

Square made the game as a sequel to Hideaki Sena’s novel, blending role-playing combat with horror and elaborate computer-generated scenes.

It is an odd mixture of science fiction, action and body horror, with a memorable score by Yoko Shimomura. Its opening scene at the opera remains one of the most startling in any game of its time.
