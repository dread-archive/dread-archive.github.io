DA-0364 Folklore · 2000

The Hands Resist Him

Bill Stoneham (painting); eBay listing · United States (internet)

Dread 3/5

A painting of a boy and a doll in front of a glass door, sold online with a warning that it moved at night.

In 2000, a painting appeared for sale on eBay. It showed a young boy and a doll-like girl standing in front of a glass-paned door, with small hands pressed against the glass behind them. The sellers claimed that the children in the painting moved at night, and that people who looked at it felt unwell. The listing spread across the internet, and the painting sold.

The artist, Bill Stoneham, had painted it in 1972, based on a photograph of himself as a child. He said he had heard no stories of it being haunted before the listing.

It is considered one of the first internet legends, and it depended on the new medium: a single photograph, a description, and thousands of strangers looking at the same image and feeling watched.
