DA-0103 Film · 1988

The Vanishing

George Sluizer · Netherlands / France

Dread 5/5

A woman disappears at a service station. Three years later, the man who took her offers to show her boyfriend what happened.

Rex and Saskia, a young Dutch couple on holiday in France, stop for petrol. She goes inside to buy drinks and never comes back. Three years later Rex is still looking. Then the man responsible, a quiet family man named Raymond Lemorne, gets in touch.

George Sluizer’s film, known in Dutch as Spoorloos, shows us Raymond early, including his careful rehearsals. It is not a mystery about who. It is about why, and what Rex will agree to in order to find out.

The ending is famous. It follows perfectly from the one thing Rex cannot give up: needing to know.
