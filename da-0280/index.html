DA-0280 Game · 2014

DreadOut

Digital Happiness · Indonesia

Dread 3/5

A schoolgirl on a trip to an abandoned town discovers that the only way to fight its ghosts is to photograph them.

Linda, a high school student, is on a field trip with her classmates and teacher when they take a detour into a deserted town. They explore its empty school, and the group begins to fall apart as ghosts appear. Linda discovers that her phone camera can reveal and drive back the spirits.

Digital Happiness, a studio from Bandung, partly crowdfunded the game. It was one of the first internationally successful games from Indonesia, and was built around ghosts from Indonesian folklore, such as the kuntilanak and pocong.

Its ghosts are its strength: unfamiliar to most foreign players and rooted in local belief. Making the player look at them through a small screen in order to survive is a simple and effective idea.
