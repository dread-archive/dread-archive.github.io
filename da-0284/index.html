DA-0284 Game · 2019

Devotion

Red Candle Games · Taiwan

Dread 5/5

A man walks through the same small Taipei apartment across the 1980s as his family comes apart around a sick child.

Du Feng Yu, a screenwriter, lives in a modest Taiwanese apartment with his wife, a former singer, and their daughter, Mei Shin, a child performer. The player walks through the flat in first person, at different years in the 1980s, as Mei Shin falls ill and Feng Yu turns to a religious cult for help.

Red Candle Games, makers of Detention, released it in February 2019. Within days it was removed from sale after players found a hidden image mocking China’s leader, and it was for years almost impossible to buy.

It is a game about a family more than a monster. Its horror is slow and domestic: the same rooms, darker each year, and a father’s devotion that becomes something no one should have to watch.
