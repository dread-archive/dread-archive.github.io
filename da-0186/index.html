DA-0186 Book · 1984

Books of Blood

Clive Barker · United Kingdom

Dread 4/5

A young man who has been faking contact with the dead finds that the dead have started writing on him.

The collection’s frame story is simple. Simon McNeal, a young medium, has been fooling a parapsychologist with false messages. The dead lose patience, and begin to carve their own stories into his skin. Each story in the books is one of them: a subway train with a butcher on board, a duel between two towns in Yugoslavia, a theatre company with an odd director.

The six volumes were published by Sphere in 1984 and 1985, before Barker became known for The Hellbound Heart and its film. Stephen King’s cover praise was much quoted.

The stories are frank about bodies, sex and pain in a way British horror had rarely been. They often end not in escape but in transformation, which is worse.
