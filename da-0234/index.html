DA-0234 Game · 2012

Lone Survivor

Jasper Byrne · United Kingdom

Dread 3/5

A man in a surgical mask has not left his apartment for weeks. The city outside is full of things that used to be people.

After a disease has torn through the city, a nameless man in a surgical mask lives alone in an apartment block. He is hungry, sleeps badly, and talks to himself. Outside, faceless creatures shuffle along the corridors. He dreams of a man in a box, and of a girl in a green dress, and is no longer sure which parts of his days are real.

Jasper Byrne made it largely alone, in a low-resolution pixel style with side-scrolling movement and a heavy influence from Silent Hill. He also wrote its music.

It is about loneliness as much as monsters. The player can feed him, let him sleep, or medicate him, and every choice shapes both his health and the reality around him.
