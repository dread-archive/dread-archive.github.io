DA-0054 Book · 1962

We Have Always Lived in the Castle

Shirley Jackson · United States

Dread 3/5

Two sisters and their uncle live apart from the village that hates them. One of them poisoned the family.

Mary Katherine Blackwood, called Merricat, is eighteen. She lives with her sister Constance and their frail uncle Julian in the family house, six years after the rest of the family died from arsenic in the sugar bowl. The village has never forgiven them. Merricat buries objects and nails them to trees to keep the house safe.

It was Shirley Jackson’s last completed novel. Merricat narrates it in a voice that is charming, childlike and increasingly alarming.

The horror is gentle and total. By the end, the sisters have what they wanted, and it is the most frightening thing in the book.
