DA-0191 Game · 1995

I Have No Mouth, and I Must Scream (game)

The Dreamers Guild / Cyberdreams, with Harlan Ellison · United States

Dread 4/5

An adventure game based on Ellison's story, in which the only way to win is to choose well.

The game adapts Harlan Ellison’s short story (DA-0034). A supercomputer called AM has destroyed humanity except for five people, whom it has kept alive and tortured for more than a century. It gives each of them a scenario built from their worst memories and guilt, and watches.

Ellison co-wrote the game and voiced AM himself. The player cannot beat AM by force. Each character’s story can only be resolved by confronting what they did, and the best ending depends on acts of sacrifice rather than skill.

It was dark and uncomfortable for its time, and remains unusual today: a game about a machine’s hatred, in which the player’s choices are moral rather than tactical.
