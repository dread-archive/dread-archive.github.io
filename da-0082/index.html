DA-0082 Film · 1990

Jacob's Ladder

Adrian Lyne · United States

Dread 4/5

A veteran in New York sees demons in ordinary faces, and cannot tell which of his lives is real.

Jacob Singer served in Vietnam and now works as a postal clerk in New York. He starts to see things: a figure in a subway car with something wrong with its face, a nurse with a horn, people whose heads shake too fast to see. He slips between his present life, his memories of a dead son, and a hospital where no one will tell him anything.

Adrian Lyne filmed the hallucinations with a simple trick of shooting people shaking their heads at a low frame rate, and it is still deeply unsettling. The film has been cited as a strong influence on the Silent Hill games (DA-0209, DA-0212).

Its ending recasts everything. It is less a twist than a kind of mercy.
