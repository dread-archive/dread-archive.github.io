DA-0153 Film · 2016

The Conjuring 2

James Wan · United States

Dread 3/5

The Warrens travel to north London, where a family's house is at the centre of a famous poltergeist case.

In 1977, in Enfield, a single mother and her four children report furniture moving on its own, knocking in the walls, and an old man’s voice speaking through one of the daughters. Ed and Lorraine Warren fly in from America to investigate, while Lorraine is troubled by visions of a figure dressed as a nun.

James Wan’s sequel is built on the same care as the first film, and on a real, much-disputed case, recorded elsewhere in this archive.

The film presents itself as based on true events. As always in this genre, that claim is part of the performance.
