DA-0347 Folklore · 1970

Bloody Mary

Traditional · United States / United Kingdom

Dread 3/5

A game children dare each other to play: a dark bathroom, a mirror, and a name said aloud.

The ritual has many versions. Usually, a child goes into a dark bathroom, stands in front of the mirror, and says a name several times, often three, often “Bloody Mary.” Then she will appear in the glass. What happens next depends on who is telling it.

Folklorists have collected the game in many forms, mostly from children at sleepovers, and have written about why it appeals at a particular age: the dare, the mirror, the name.

What gives it its power is that it requires no props and no belief, only a mirror and a dark room. Almost everyone who has played it remembers the moment before they opened their eyes.
