DA-0329 Folklore · 2007

The Holders

Unknown (collaborative) · United States (internet)

Dread 3/5

A collaborative internet series about hundreds of strange objects, each held by a figure you must question correctly.

Each entry in The Holders follows the same pattern. In any city, go to a particular institution, and ask for a particular person. You will be taken somewhere you should not be. There, you must ask a specific question exactly right. If you do, you will be given an object. There are hundreds of objects, and together they are said to form something terrible if brought together.

The series grew as many anonymous authors contributed new entries, in a shared formal style, on forums and wikis from around 2007.

It is less a story than a ritual repeated hundreds of times. Its appeal is its strict form, like a collection of instructions written by something that does not want to be found.
