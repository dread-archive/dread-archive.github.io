DA-0257 Game · 1999

Galerians

Polygon Magic · Japan

Dread 3/5

A boy wakes strapped to a hospital bed with no memory, a head full of psychic power and a voice calling him for help.

Rion Steiner, a teenage boy, wakes in a research hospital after experiments he cannot remember. A girl’s voice, Lilia’s, calls to him in his head. He escapes using psychic abilities that the doctors have given him, and searches for her through the hospital, his family home and a hotel run by strange, artificial young people called the Galerians.

Polygon Magic developed it for the original PlayStation, with long computer-generated cutscenes that were ambitious for the time. Rion has no guns; he fights with telekinesis and fire, powered by injections.

The drugs are the game’s real horror. Rion must keep dosing himself to fight, but too much sends him into a fit that damages everything nearby, and him with it. Its story of children used as material for a machine is bleak and cold.
