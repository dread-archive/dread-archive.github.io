DA-0335 Broadcast · 2016

The Magnus Archives

Jonathan Sims (Rusty Quill) · United Kingdom

Dread 4/5

A new head archivist at a London institute records old statements about the supernatural, one per episode.

Jonathan Sims is the new Head Archivist of the Magnus Institute in London, an organisation that researches the paranormal. Its archives are in chaos, full of written statements from people describing encounters they could not explain. He begins recording them on tape, one by one.

It was written by Jonathan Sims, who also plays the Archivist, and produced by Rusty Quill with Alexander J. Newall. It ran for five seasons and two hundred episodes, from 2016 to 2021.

It begins as an anthology, with each episode a separate story of horror in an ordinary setting. Slowly, the stories begin to connect, and to concern the archive itself. Its central fear is of being looked at, catalogued and understood by something that should not know you.
