DA-0201 Game · 2017

Darkwood

Acid Wizard Studio · Poland

Dread 5/5

By day you search the forest. By night you barricade yourself in and wait.

A forest somewhere in Eastern Europe has grown over the roads and trapped the people inside it. The player, seen from above, explores by day to find materials, trade with strange locals and make sense of what has happened. At night, everything that lives in the woods comes out, and the only choice is to board up a hideout, light a generator and wait for morning.

The small Polish studio built the game around sight: you only see what your character can see, in a cone of light, and everything outside it is dark.

Its nights are among the most frightening in any game, largely because very little happens in them. Something tries a door. Something moves in the next room. Morning is a long way off.
