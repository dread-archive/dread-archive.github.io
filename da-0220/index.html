DA-0220 Game · 2003

Fatal Frame II: Crimson Butterfly

Tecmo · Japan

Dread 5/5

Twin sisters wander into a lost village where a terrible ritual never finished. Your only weapon is a camera.

Two twin sisters, Mio and Mayu, visit a place from their childhood that is about to be flooded by a dam. Following a red butterfly, Mayu leads them into a village that should not exist, where it is always dusk and the ghosts of its villagers are still acting out a ritual that went wrong.

Like the first Fatal Frame (DA-0219), the game’s only defence is an antique camera that can photograph and damage spirits, which forces the player to look straight at what they are most afraid of.

It is widely considered the best in the series and one of the scariest games ever made. Its real subject is the bond between the sisters, and the ritual’s cruel demand that one of them lose the other.
