DA-0408 Film · 2025

Weapons

Zach Cregger · United States

Dread 4/5

One night at the same minute, seventeen children from one class leave their homes and run into the dark.

In a small town, every child in one school class except one wakes at exactly 2:17 in the morning, walks out of their home, and runs into the night, arms held out at their sides. Home cameras record it. None of them come back. The town turns on their teacher, the one adult connected to all of them.

Zach Cregger, who made Barbarian (DA-0387), tells the story in overlapping chapters, each from the point of view of a different person in the town, with each chapter revealing a little more.

It is funny, frightening and increasingly strange. The image of the children running into the dark at the same minute, filmed on doorbell cameras, is one of the defining horror images of its year.
