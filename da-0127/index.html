DA-0127 Film · 1999

The Blair Witch Project

Daniel Myrick & Eduardo Sánchez · United States

Dread 3/5

The film that made the audience wonder, for one summer, whether it was allowed to be real.

In 1994, three student filmmakers walked into the woods near Burkittsville, Maryland, to make a documentary about a local legend. The film presents itself as the footage they left behind.

The actors improvised much of their dialogue and operated the cameras themselves. Before release, a website presented the disappearance as a real case, with police reports and missing-person details, and many viewers arrived unsure whether they were watching fiction.

Almost nothing is shown. There are sounds outside a tent at night, piles of stones, and a map that should not be lost. The film turned the found-footage format into a genre, and proved how much an audience will do for you if you leave it enough room.
