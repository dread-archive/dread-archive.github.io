DA-0204 Game · 1992

Dark Seed

Cyberdreams, with artwork by H. R. Giger · United States

Dread 3/5

A writer moves into an old house and dreams of a machine putting something into his head.

Mike Dawson, a writer, buys a Victorian house and on his first night has a nightmare of being strapped into a machine. He wakes with a headache that does not go away. Exploring, he finds that a mirror leads to a dark version of his world, built from bones and machinery, where an alien race has implanted a seed in him. He has three days.

The game was famous above all for its art. H. R. Giger, the Swiss artist behind Alien, licensed his work to Cyberdreams, who built the dark world in high resolution.

It is a punishing point-and-click adventure, easy to make impossible to finish. Yet its two worlds, one homely and one biomechanical, are uncomfortably close, and every room in Mike’s house has a double.
