DA-0294 Game · 2019

Song of Horror

Protocol Games · Spain

Dread 4/5

A famous novelist and his family have vanished. Everyone who goes looking for them hears the same music.

The writer Sebastian P. Husher has disappeared, along with his wife and daughter, from their house. His publisher sends an assistant, Daniel Noble, to find out what happened. Inside the house, a music box plays a tune, and everyone who hears it becomes the target of something called the Presence.

Protocol Games, a small Spanish studio, made it as an episodic game with fixed camera angles, in the tradition of the early survival horror games. Its first episodes appeared in 2019.

The player chooses from a group of characters, and any of them can die permanently. The Presence is not scripted in a fixed way: it can appear behind any door, at any time. Listening at a door before opening it becomes a ritual, and every corridor a gamble.
