DA-0330 Folklore · 2010

Lavender Town Syndrome

Anonymous (online) · Internet

Dread 2/5

A legend that the music of a town in the first Pokémon games drove Japanese children to illness and suicide.

In the original Pokémon games, released in Japan in 1996, Lavender Town is a small settlement with a tower full of graves for dead Pokémon. Its background music is a high, repetitive, slightly dissonant melody. The legend says that after the games came out, many children who heard it suffered headaches, nosebleeds and worse.

The story circulated widely online from around 2010, on forums and paste sites, and grew with each retelling: secret frequencies, hidden messages, hospitals filling up. There is no evidence that any of it happened.

It frightens because the music really is strange, and because it attaches a horror to something many people played as children. The tune can be found and listened to, which is exactly what the story dares its readers to do.
