DA-0121 Film · 2015

Hell House LLC

Stephen Cognetti · United States

Dread 4/5

A haunted-house attraction opens in an abandoned hotel. On opening night, something goes wrong for everyone inside.

A group of friends who build Halloween attractions rent the Abaddon Hotel in a small town in New York State. During the weeks of setting up, a clown mannequin keeps appearing in places no one left it. Opening night ends with many people dead, and nobody explains why.

Stephen Cognetti tells it as a documentary made years later, built from the crew’s own behind-the-scenes footage. It was released cheaply online and spread largely by word of mouth.

Its fear comes from stillness. The mannequins never move on camera. They just aren’t where they were a moment ago.
