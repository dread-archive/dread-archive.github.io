DA-0032 Book · 1910

The Wendigo

Algernon Blackwood · England / Canada

Dread 4/5

A hunting trip in the northern Canadian forest, and a name called out in the night.

A small hunting party goes deep into the forests of northern Canada, far from any settlement. One of the guides, Défago, is uneasy from the start, and the smell and silence of the woods seem to affect him more than the others. One night, something outside the tent calls his name. He runs into the dark. In the morning his tracks change as they go, the steps growing longer and further apart.

Algernon Blackwood knew the Canadian wilderness well, and the story’s power comes from its sense of scale: the forest is so large that the men are small in it, and something in it has noticed them.

Blackwood never shows the creature. He shows what it does to the one who is taken, and to those who have to watch him come back.
