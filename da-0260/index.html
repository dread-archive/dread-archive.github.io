DA-0260 Game · 1996

Corpse Party

Makoto Kedouin (Team GrisGris) · Japan

Dread 4/5

Students perform a friendship charm in their school after hours and wake up in an elementary school that was demolished years ago.

On the night of a school festival, a group of friends at Kisaragi Academy perform a charm with a paper doll so that they will always be together. The building shakes, the floor opens, and they wake, separated, in Heavenly Host Elementary, a decayed wooden school full of corpses and the ghosts of murdered children.

Makoto Kedouin first made it for the Japanese PC-98 computer using RPG-making software. It was rebuilt in 2008 and then expanded for the PSP, which brought it a much larger audience and a series of sequels.

Its sprites are small and cute, which makes its descriptions of violence, written out in plain text, more shocking. Characters can die permanently, and the game makes clear how easily a friendship charm can become a curse.
