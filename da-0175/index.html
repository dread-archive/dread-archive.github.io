DA-0175 Film · 2016

Raw

Julia Ducournau · France / Belgium

Dread 4/5

A vegetarian student is made to eat a raw rabbit kidney in her first week at veterinary school, and something in her wakes up.

Justine, sixteen, clever and strictly vegetarian like the rest of her family, arrives at the veterinary school where her older sister Alexia is already a student. The hazing rituals begin at once: blood thrown over the new intake, nights of forced parties, and a raw rabbit kidney she is pressured to swallow. Soon she has a rash, and then a craving she cannot satisfy with anything from the canteen.

Julia Ducournau’s first feature, known in French as Grave, premiered at Cannes in 2016. It became notorious when audience members were reported to have fainted at a festival screening in Toronto.

The film treats its horror as a coming of age. Justine’s hunger is frightening less for what it makes her do than for how familiar it seems to the people who love her.
