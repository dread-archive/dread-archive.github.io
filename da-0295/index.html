DA-0295 Game · 2012

Home

Benjamin Rivers · Canada

Dread 3/5

A man wakes in a stranger's house beside a body, and walks home through the dark with a torch, trying to understand.

A man wakes up in an unfamiliar house with no memory of the night before. There is a dead body in the room. He has a torch, a vague worry about his wife, and a long walk ahead through a sewer, a forest and empty buildings to get back to his own home.

Benjamin Rivers made it almost entirely alone, in small pixel art with very little sound. It takes about an hour and a half.

The game asks small questions as the player goes, about whether to pick something up or look at something. At the end, it asks the player what they think happened, and leaves it to them. Its horror is the gap between what the narrator says and what the player has seen, and the suspicion that he is not telling everything.
