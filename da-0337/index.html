DA-0337 Folklore · 2007

SCP Foundation

Anonymous (online, collaborative) · Internet

Dread 3/5

The internal files of a secret organisation that captures and contains anomalous objects, written in the flat language of procedure.

Each entry describes an anomaly, numbered SCP-something, and explains how it must be held: its “Special Containment Procedures”, then its description, then incident logs and redacted interviews. Some are statues that move when unobserved, others are rooms, ideas, songs or entire towns.

It began with a single post in 2007 on 4chan’s paranormal board describing SCP-173, a concrete statue that kills when no one is looking. A dedicated wiki followed, and thousands of writers have since added entries.

Its style is bureaucratic, and that is its genius. The horror is never fully shown. It is implied by blacked-out words, staff losses listed in passing, and the sense of a world held together by people following rules about things that cannot be understood.
