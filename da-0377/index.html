DA-0377 Folklore · 2019

The Backrooms

Anonymous (4chan /x/); later Kane Parsons · Internet

Dread 4/5

A single photograph of an empty office became an entire geography.

In 2019, on an image board thread asking for pictures that “just feel off,” someone posted a photograph of an empty room with yellow wallpaper, damp carpet and fluorescent lights. A reply described it: if you are not careful and you “noclip” out of reality, you end up here.

The idea spread. People wrote levels, rules and entities. In 2022, a teenage filmmaker named Kane Parsons made a series of short found-footage videos that gave the Backrooms a history, and a huge new audience.

The horror is ordinary space with no one in it: an office after everyone has gone home, extended forever. It works on anyone who has ever been in a building too late.
