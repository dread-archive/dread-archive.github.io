DA-0139 Film · 2001

Pulse (Kairo)

Kiyoshi Kurosawa · Japan

Dread 5/5

The most frightening film about the internet, and the loneliest.

In Tokyo, people begin disappearing. Before they go, some of them find a website that asks a simple question: would you like to meet a ghost? Some of them find rooms sealed with red tape and do not stay out of them.

Kurosawa’s ghosts do not attack. They stand in corners and move slightly wrong. What they want is harder to accept than violence. The dead, the film suggests, are simply alone, and the network lets their loneliness through.

Pulse was made at the start of the always-connected age and predicted its mood with unsettling precision: millions of people in contact with each other and none of them less isolated. The last act empties a city. It never raises its voice.
