DA-0050 Book · 1897

The Beetle

Richard Marsh · United Kingdom

Dread 3/5

A shape-changing creature follows a rising politician through London. In its year, it outsold *Dracula*.

A starving clerk climbs through the window of an empty house in London and finds something waiting in the bed. It speaks, it commands, and it sends him out to steal letters from Paul Lessingham, a respected member of Parliament with something to hide from a trip to Egypt years before.

Richard Marsh tells the story through four narrators, each of whom sees only part of the creature, which appears as a man, a woman and an enormous beetle. It was a bestseller in 1897, the same year Dracula was published, and then it was largely forgotten.

It is uneven and full of the prejudices of its time. Its best chapters, the ones in the dark house, are genuinely nightmarish.
