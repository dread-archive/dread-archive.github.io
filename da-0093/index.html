DA-0093 Film · 2008

Let the Right One In

Tomas Alfredson · Sweden

Dread 3/5

A bullied boy befriends the strange girl next door. A vampire story that is really about loneliness.

In a snowy suburb of Stockholm in the early 1980s, Oskar, a lonely twelve-year-old, is bullied at school and dreams of revenge. One night he meets Eli, the girl who has just moved in next door. She does not feel the cold. She only comes out at night. They talk through the wall in Morse code. Meanwhile, bodies are being found around the neighbourhood.

Tomas Alfredson adapted John Ajvide Lindqvist’s novel with great restraint: long, quiet, snowy shots, and sudden violence that is shown briefly and from a distance.

It is a tender film and a disturbing one, because it never tells you whether the friendship at its centre is a rescue or a trap.
