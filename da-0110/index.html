DA-0110 Film · 1997

Cube

Vincenzo Natali · Canada

Dread 3/5

Six strangers wake in a maze of identical rooms, some of them trapped. Nobody remembers arriving.

Six people wake up inside a cube-shaped room with a hatch in each wall. Each hatch leads to another identical room, in a different colour. Some of the rooms kill whoever enters. None of the six remember how they got there, or know why they were chosen.

Vincenzo Natali made the film on a single set that was redressed to look like many rooms. The group includes a police officer, a doctor, a student with a talent for mathematics, and a man who may have helped design an outer shell without knowing what it was for.

Its bleakest idea is that nobody is in charge. The maze exists because a system built it, and nobody stopped to ask why.
