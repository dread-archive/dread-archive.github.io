DA-0277 Game · 2012

The Witch's House

Fummy · Japan

Dread 3/5

A girl wakes in a forest, finds her way home blocked by roses, and goes into the only house nearby.

Viola, a thirteen-year-old girl, wakes in a forest clearing. The path back to her home is closed by a thicket of roses that will not let her through. The only other way leads into a house, which, according to a black cat she meets, belongs to a witch. Inside, the walls and objects can kill her.

Fummy, a Japanese solo developer, made it with the free Wolf RPG Editor. It is short and full of puzzles, and like other free Japanese horror games of the period it spread widely through translations and online videos. A remake appeared in 2018.

The house is its antagonist. Sudden deaths, a teddy bear with the wrong expression and messages on the walls teach the player to distrust everything. Its ending is famous, and best met without warning.
