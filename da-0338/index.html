DA-0338 Broadcast · 2019

Hellier

Greg and Dana Newkirk, Karl Pfeiffer · United States

Dread 2/5

A group of paranormal investigators follow emails about goblins in rural Kentucky, and find something stranger than they expected.

In 2012, paranormal researchers Greg and Dana Newkirk received emails from a man describing small creatures emerging from a mine near the town of Hellier, Kentucky. Years later, the sender could not be traced. With friends and a film crew, they travelled to Kentucky to see what they could find.

The documentary series was directed by Karl Pfeiffer and released online in 2019, with a second season later the same year. It is unscripted.

It is not frightening in the usual way. There are no clear monsters. Instead, the investigators follow a chain of coincidences, numbers and names that seem to point somewhere. Its unease is in watching sensible people become more uncertain about where the strangeness is coming from.
