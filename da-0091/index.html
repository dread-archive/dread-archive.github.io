DA-0091 Film · 2001

The Devil's Backbone

Guillermo del Toro · Spain / Mexico

Dread 3/5

A ghost story set in an orphanage during the Spanish Civil War, where the living are the danger.

Carlos, a young boy whose father has died in the Spanish Civil War, is left at a remote orphanage run by supporters of the Republic. An unexploded bomb stands in the courtyard. The older boys whisper about a ghost they call the one who sighs. The caretaker, Jacinto, a former orphan himself, wants something hidden in the building.

The film opens by asking what a ghost is, and answers: a moment repeated, an emotion held in time. It follows that idea. The ghost is frightening, but its wish is simple and sad.

It is the first of del Toro’s films to place a ghost story inside the Spanish Civil War, a pairing he returned to in Pan’s Labyrinth. The real threat is human, and it is standing in the courtyard all along.
