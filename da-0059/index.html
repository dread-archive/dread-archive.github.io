DA-0059 Film · 1962

Carnival of Souls

Herk Harvey · United States

Dread 4/5

A low-budget independent film that became a cult classic, and a quiet influence on half of modern horror.

Mary Henry climbs out of the river after a car goes off a bridge. She seems unhurt. She moves to a new town to work as a church organist, but something is wrong. People sometimes cannot see or hear her. A pale man keeps appearing in windows and reflections. She is drawn to an abandoned lakeside pavilion.

Herk Harvey, who made industrial and educational films, shot this independently on a tiny budget and played the pale man himself. The film was barely noticed on release and gained its reputation later on television and in revival screenings.

Its strength is its mood of disconnection: an ordinary woman slowly realising she no longer quite fits in the world. The organ score and the empty pavilion give it the feel of a bad dream remembered by daylight.
