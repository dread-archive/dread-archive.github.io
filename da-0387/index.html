DA-0387 Film · 2022

Barbarian

Zach Cregger · United States

Dread 4/5

A woman arrives at a rental house in Detroit to find it double-booked. The basement goes further than it should.

Tess arrives late on a rainy night at a house she has rented in a run-down area of Detroit, and finds that a man called Keith is already staying there. It seems to be a mix-up. She reluctantly agrees to stay. In the basement, she finds a hidden door, and behind it a corridor that should not exist.

Zach Cregger’s film is best known for its sudden, bold shifts in time, place and tone, which keep the audience permanently off balance. It moves from a tense stranger-danger story to something much stranger and more grotesque.

Knowing as little as possible is the best way to watch it. Its structure is the scare: every time you think you know what kind of film this is, a new door opens.
