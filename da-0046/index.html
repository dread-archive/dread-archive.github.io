DA-0046 Film · 1957

Night of the Demon

Jacques Tourneur · United Kingdom

Dread 4/5

A sceptical psychologist is handed a slip of paper covered in runes, and told he has three days.

Dr John Holden, an American psychologist, arrives in England to expose Julian Karswell, a charming cult leader. A colleague who crossed Karswell has just died. Then Holden finds a small parchment slipped among his papers, and a date by which he will die.

Adapted from M. R. James’s story Casting the Runes, the film was released in the United States as Curse of the Demon. The producer insisted on showing the demon; Tourneur had wanted to leave it in the dark. Viewers still argue about who was right.

The idea at its centre is simple and old: a curse that can only be escaped by giving it back to the person who gave it to you, without them noticing.
