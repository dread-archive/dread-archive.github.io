DA-0323 Folklore · 2010

Ben Drowned

Alexander D. Hall · United States (internet)

Dread 3/5

A college student buys a used Zelda cartridge from an old man. The game is haunted, and it has been waiting.

Posted online in 2010 by a user calling himself Jadusable, the story tells of a college student who buys a used copy of The Legend of Zelda: Majora’s Mask from a strange old man at a garage sale. The cartridge contains a save file named BEN. The game begins to behave impossibly, and a statue follows the player around.

What made it spread was the format: the story was told through forum posts, then through videos of the haunted game, and the author answered readers in character. It became an early alternate reality game, with a mystery that unfolded in real time.

It is a story about a game that holds on to the last person who played it, and it was an important early example of horror told across many platforms at once.
