DA-0052 Book · 1975

'Salem's Lot

Stephen King · United States

Dread 4/5

Dracula comes to a small town in Maine, and the town dies one house at a time.

A writer returns to the small Maine town where he spent part of his childhood, planning a book about the Marsten House, a mansion on the hill where something terrible happened. The house has just been bought by two strangers who are opening an antique shop. A boy disappears. His brother dies. Then the dead start coming back.

Stephen King’s second novel sets out to imagine what would happen if Dracula arrived in modern small-town America. His answer is that the town’s own secrets, gossip and indifference would help him.

The book’s best scenes are small: a child’s face at a bedroom window, a knocking on the glass, a friend asking to be let in. The town does not end with a battle. It simply empties.
