DA-0147 Film · 2016

The Wailing

Na Hong-jin · South Korea

Dread 5/5

A clumsy village policeman investigates a wave of violent illness. Everybody blames the Japanese stranger in the hills.

In the mountain village of Gokseong, people fall ill with rashes and rage, and then kill their families. Jong-goo, an easily frightened local policeman, hears that it began when a Japanese man moved into a hut in the woods. When his own daughter starts to change, he calls a shaman.

Na Hong-jin’s film is two and a half hours long, and moves between comedy, procedural and religious horror. It gives several people authority, and asks Jong-goo to decide whom to believe.

The film’s last hour is a set of competing explanations, each convincing. Believing the wrong one is the whole danger.
