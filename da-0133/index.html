DA-0133 Film · 2012

The Cabin in the Woods

Drew Goddard (written with Joss Whedon) · United States

Dread 2/5

Five friends go to a cabin for the weekend. Behind the glass, technicians are making sure the story goes to plan.

Five college students drive to a remote cabin. Meanwhile, in an underground facility, two cheerful technicians watch them on screens, place bets, and nudge events along with chemicals and hidden switches. Everything the students do has been arranged.

Drew Goddard’s film is a comedy about horror films, and a sharp one. It explains, with bureaucratic detail, why characters in horror always split up and go into the basement.

Behind the jokes is a real unease: the idea that a story has an audience you cannot see, and that the audience needs you to follow the script.
