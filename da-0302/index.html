DA-0302 Broadcast · 1958

Quatermass and the Pit

Nigel Kneale (BBC) · United Kingdom

Dread 4/5

Workers digging in London find something buried that is much older than it should be, and it still has influence.

During building work in a London street, workers uncover ancient human remains, and then a strange object that the army assumes is an unexploded bomb. Professor Bernard Quatermass is not so sure. The street has a long history of hauntings, and the object seems to trigger visions in the people near it.

Nigel Kneale’s serial was broadcast live on the BBC in six parts and gripped the country. It connects science fiction, archaeology and folklore to suggest that humanity’s oldest fears, of devils and of the other, might be a memory of something real.

It was remade as a film by Hammer in 1967. The original serial is slow by modern standards, but its central idea, that something buried can still shape the people who walk over it, is as unsettling as ever.
